am・is・are

be 動詞：am、is、are

The verb be: am, is, are — English grammar, junior high

接續

I am・you／we／they are・he／she／it is　縮寫：I'm・you're・he's・it's
I am · you / we / they are · he / she / it is · short forms: I'm, you're, he's, it's

說明

be 動詞是「是、在」，跟著主詞變化：I am a student.（我是學生。）She is in the library.（她在圖書館。）否定在後面加 not，疑問句把 be 動詞移到句首：Are you OK?

be means “is / are” and changes with the subject: I am a student, She is in the library. Add not after it for the negative, and put it first for a question: Are you OK?

注意事項

  • 複數主詞（包含 A and B）用 are：Tom and I are friends.
    Plural subjects, including A and B, take are: Tom and I are friends.
  • 否定縮寫：isn't、aren't，但 am not 沒有縮寫成 amn't。
    Negative short forms: isn't, aren't — but there's no amn't.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ My brother are tall.
    ✓ My brother is tall.
    my brother 是單數，用 is。
    my brother is singular: is.
  • ✗ I tired.
    ✓ I am tired.
    形容詞前面不能少了 be 動詞。
    You can't leave out be before an adjective.

例句

I'm from Taiwan.
我來自台灣。
Is your father a teacher?
你爸爸是老師嗎？
We aren't late.
我們沒有遲到。
