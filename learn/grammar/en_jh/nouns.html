可數名詞、不可數名詞和複數

Countable and uncountable nouns — English grammar, junior high

接續

可數：a／an ＋ 單數、複數 -s／-es　不可數：water、milk、money（不加 -s、不加 a）
countable: a / an + singular, plural -s / -es · uncountable: water, milk, money (no -s, no a)

說明

能一個一個數的名詞是可數名詞，單數前面要有 a／an，複數加 -s：a pen、three pens。不可數名詞（液體、材料、抽象的東西）沒有複數，要用單位來數：a glass of milk、two pieces of paper。

Countable nouns take a / an or -s: a pen, three pens. Uncountable nouns (liquids, materials, ideas) have no plural; count them with units: a glass of milk.

注意事項

  • some 用在肯定句，any 用在否定句和疑問句：I have some cookies. ／ Do you have any questions?
    some in statements, any in negatives and questions: I have some cookies. / Do you have any questions?
  • 不規則複數：man → men、child → children、foot → feet、tooth → teeth。
    Irregular plurals: men, children, feet, teeth.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ I need some informations.
    ✓ I need some information.
    information 是不可數名詞，沒有 -s。
    information is uncountable: no -s.
  • ✗ There are three childs.
    ✓ There are three children.
    child 的複數是 children。
    child's plural is children.

例句

Can I have a glass of water?
可以給我一杯水嗎？
She has two dogs and a cat.
她有兩隻狗和一隻貓。
We don't have any milk.
我們沒有牛奶了。
