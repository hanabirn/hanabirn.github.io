There is・There are

There is／There are：有

There is / There are — English grammar, junior high

接續

There is ＋ 單數／不可數名詞・There are ＋ 複數名詞　疑問：Is there …? ／ Are there …?
There is + singular / uncountable · There are + plural · questions: Is there …? / Are there …?

說明

說「某處有某物」用 there is／are，be 動詞跟著後面的名詞變：There is a park near my home.（我家附近有一座公園。）There are thirty students in my class.

To say something exists somewhere, use there is / are, matching the noun after it: There is a park near my home, There are thirty students in my class.

注意事項

  • 「有」不要用 have 來說存在：There is a cat under the table.，不說 It has a cat under the table。
    Don't use have for existence: There is a cat under the table.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ There is many people here.
    ✓ There are many people here.
    people 是複數，用 are。
    people is plural: are.
  • ✗ There have a bank near here.
    ✓ There is a bank near here.
    there 後面接 be 動詞，不接 have。
    there is followed by be, not have.

例句

There is a convenience store on the corner.
轉角有一間便利商店。
Are there any eggs in the fridge?
冰箱裡有蛋嗎？
There isn't much time.
時間不多了。
