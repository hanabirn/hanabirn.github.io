must・have to・should

必須和應該：must、have to、should

must, have to, should — English grammar, junior high

接續

must ＋ 原形（必須）・have／has to ＋ 原形（必須、不得不）・should ＋ 原形（應該）　mustn't（不准）・don't have to（不必）
must + verb · have / has to + verb · should + verb · mustn't (not allowed) · don't have to (not necessary)

說明

must 和 have to 都是「必須」：You must wear a helmet.、I have to get up early. should 是建議「應該」：You should drink more water. 注意否定意思不同：You mustn't park here.（這裡不准停車。）You don't have to come.（你不必來。）

must and have to both mean “need to”: You must wear a helmet, I have to get up early. should gives advice: You should drink more water. The negatives differ: mustn't = not allowed, don't have to = not necessary.

注意事項

  • must 沒有過去式，過去的「必須」用 had to：I had to wait an hour.
    must has no past; use had to: I had to wait an hour.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ She must to go now.
    ✓ She must go now.
    must 後面直接接原形。
    must takes the base form directly.
  • ✗ He have to work today.
    ✓ He has to work today.
    he 用 has to。
    With he it's has to.

例句

You should see a doctor.
你應該去看醫生。
Students must not use phones in class.
學生上課不准用手機。
We don't have to wear uniforms on Fridays.
我們星期五不必穿制服。
