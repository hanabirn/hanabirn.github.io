…, isn't it?

附加問句

Tag questions — English grammar, junior high

接續

肯定句 ＋ 否定附加問句（It's hot, isn't it?）・否定句 ＋ 肯定附加問句（You don't eat pork, do you?）
positive sentence + negative tag (It's hot, isn't it?) · negative sentence + positive tag (You don't eat pork, do you?)

說明

在句子後面加一個短問句來確認：前面肯定、後面就否定，反之亦然。附加問句用前面的助動詞或 be 動詞，一般動詞就用 do／does／did，主詞換成代名詞：Your sister lives in Taichung, doesn't she?

A short question at the end checks something: a positive sentence takes a negative tag and vice versa. Use the same helping verb or be — or do / does / did — with a pronoun: Your sister lives in Taichung, doesn't she?

注意事項

  • I am 的附加問句是 aren't I：I'm right, aren't I?
    I am takes aren't I: I'm right, aren't I?
  • Let's … 的附加問句是 shall we：Let's go, shall we?
    Let's … takes shall we: Let's go, shall we?

常見錯誤

  • ✗ You are a student, are you?
    ✓ You are a student, aren't you?
    前面肯定，附加問句要否定。
    A positive sentence takes a negative tag.
  • ✗ He likes music, isn't he?
    ✓ He likes music, doesn't he?
    一般動詞用 does 來問。
    An ordinary verb takes does.

例句

It's a beautiful day, isn't it?
今天天氣真好，對吧？
You didn't forget the tickets, did you?
你沒忘記帶票吧？
They can come, can't they?
他們可以來，對吧？
