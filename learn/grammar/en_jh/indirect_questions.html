Do you know where … is?

間接問句

Indirect questions — English grammar, junior high

接續

Do you know／I don't know／Can you tell me ＋ 疑問詞 ＋ 主詞 ＋ 動詞（直述句語序）・沒有疑問詞時用 if／whether
Do you know / I don't know / Can you tell me + question word + subject + verb (statement order) · no question word: if / whether

說明

問句放在另一個句子裡時，要改成直述句的語序，不再倒裝：Where is the station? → Do you know where the station is? 一般動詞的 do／does 也要拿掉：What does he want? → I don't know what he wants.

A question inside another sentence goes back to statement order: Where is the station? → Do you know where the station is? Drop do / does too: What does he want? → I don't know what he wants.

注意事項

  • 是非問句改成間接問句用 if：Is she coming? → I'm not sure if she is coming.
    Yes / no questions use if: Is she coming? → I'm not sure if she is coming.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ Do you know where is the bathroom?
    ✓ Do you know where the bathroom is?
    間接問句用直述句語序。
    Indirect questions use statement order.
  • ✗ I don't know what does she like.
    ✓ I don't know what she likes.
    拿掉 does，動詞加 -s。
    Drop does and add -s to the verb.

例句

Can you tell me how much this costs?
可以告訴我這個多少錢嗎？
I wonder why he is so quiet today.
我在想他今天為什麼這麼安靜。
Do you know if the library is open?
你知道圖書館有沒有開嗎？
