played・went・did

過去簡單式

The past simple — English grammar, junior high

接續

規則動詞 ＋ -ed（play → played、stop → stopped、study → studied）・不規則（go → went、eat → ate、see → saw）　否定：didn't ＋ 原形　疑問：Did ＋ 主詞 ＋ 原形…？
regular verb + -ed (played, stopped, studied) · irregular (went, ate, saw) · negative: didn't + verb · question: Did + subject + verb …?

說明

說過去發生、已經結束的事用過去式：I visited my grandparents last weekend.（我上週末去看爺爺奶奶。）過去式不分人稱。否定和疑問用 did，後面的動詞回到原形。

For finished past events: I visited my grandparents last weekend. The past is the same for every subject. Negatives and questions use did with the base form.

注意事項

  • 常見不規則動詞：have → had、make → made、buy → bought、take → took、come → came、write → wrote。
    Common irregulars: had, made, bought, took, came, wrote.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ I didn't went to the party.
    ✓ I didn't go to the party.
    didn't 後面用原形。
    After didn't use the base form.
  • ✗ She buyed a new bag.
    ✓ She bought a new bag.
    buy 的過去式是 bought。
    buy's past is bought.

例句

We watched a movie last night.
我們昨晚看了一部電影。
Did you finish your homework?
你功課寫完了嗎？
He lost his wallet on the bus.
他在公車上弄丟了錢包。
