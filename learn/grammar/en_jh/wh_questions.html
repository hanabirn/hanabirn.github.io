What・Where・When・Who・Why・How

疑問詞問句

Wh- questions — English grammar, junior high

接續

疑問詞 ＋ be 動詞 ＋ 主詞…？　疑問詞 ＋ do／does ＋ 主詞 ＋ 原形…？
question word + be + subject …? · question word + do / does + subject + verb …?

說明

疑問詞放句首，後面接一般的疑問句語序：Where is the bathroom?、What do you want for dinner? 常用的還有 How many（多少個）、How much（多少錢）、How old（幾歲）、How often（多常）。

Put the question word first, then normal question order: Where is the bathroom?, What do you want for dinner? Also: How many, How much, How old, How often.

注意事項

  • 問「誰」當主詞時不用 do：Who lives here?（誰住這裡？）
    When who is the subject, no do: Who lives here?

常見錯誤

  • ✗ Where you live?
    ✓ Where do you live?
    一般動詞的疑問句要有 do／does。
    Questions with ordinary verbs need do / does.
  • ✗ How many money do you have?
    ✓ How much money do you have?
    money 不可數，用 how much。
    money is uncountable: how much.

例句

What time does the movie start?
電影幾點開始？
How often do you go swimming?
你多久去游泳一次？
Why are you so happy today?
你今天為什麼這麼開心？
