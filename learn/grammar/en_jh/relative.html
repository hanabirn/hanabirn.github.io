who・which・that

關係代名詞 who、which、that

Relative pronouns: who, which, that — English grammar, junior high

接續

人 ＋ who／that ＋ …・東西 ＋ which／that ＋ …
person + who / that + … · thing + which / that + …

說明

關係代名詞把兩個句子合成一句，用來說明前面的名詞是哪一個：I have a friend who lives in Canada.（我有一個住在加拿大的朋友。）This is the cake that my mom made.（這是我媽媽做的蛋糕。）

A relative pronoun joins two sentences to say which person or thing you mean: I have a friend who lives in Canada, This is the cake that my mom made.

注意事項

  • 關係代名詞當受詞時可以省略：This is the cake (that) my mom made.
    When it's the object it can be left out: This is the cake (that) my mom made.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ The man which helped me was kind.
    ✓ The man who helped me was kind.
    說人用 who 或 that，不用 which。
    For people use who or that, not which.
  • ✗ I like the dress who you bought.
    ✓ I like the dress (that) you bought.
    說東西用 which 或 that。
    For things use which or that.

例句

The girl who sits next to me is from Japan.
坐我旁邊的女生是日本人。
This is the bus that goes to the zoo.
這是開往動物園的公車。
I lost the book which you lent me.
我把你借我的書弄丟了。
