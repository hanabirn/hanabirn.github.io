always・usually・often・sometimes・never

頻率副詞

Adverbs of frequency — English grammar, junior high

接續

always（總是）> usually（通常）> often（常常）> sometimes（有時）> seldom（很少）> never（從不）　位置：一般動詞前、be 動詞後
always > usually > often > sometimes > seldom > never · before ordinary verbs, after be

說明

頻率副詞放在一般動詞前面（I usually walk to school.），但放在 be 動詞和助動詞後面（She is always late.、I can never remember.）。

They go before ordinary verbs (I usually walk to school) but after be and helping verbs (She is always late, I can never remember).

注意事項

  • never 本身就是否定，不再加 not：I never drink coffee.
    never is already negative: I never drink coffee.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ He goes always to bed late.
    ✓ He always goes to bed late.
    頻率副詞放在一般動詞前面。
    Frequency adverbs go before ordinary verbs.
  • ✗ I don't never eat breakfast.
    ✓ I never eat breakfast.
    never 不能再和 not 一起用。
    Don't use never with not.

例句

I often read before bed.
我常常睡前看書。
My dad is never angry.
我爸爸從來不生氣。
We sometimes eat at the night market.
我們有時候去夜市吃東西。
