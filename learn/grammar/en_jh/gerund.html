V-ing（動名詞）

動名詞 V-ing

Gerunds (verb-ing as a noun) — English grammar, junior high

接續

enjoy／finish／mind／keep／practice ＋ V-ing・介系詞 ＋ V-ing（good at drawing）・V-ing 當主詞（Swimming is fun.）
enjoy / finish / mind / keep / practice + verb-ing · preposition + verb-ing (good at drawing) · verb-ing as subject (Swimming is fun.)

說明

動詞加 -ing 可以當名詞用：Reading is my hobby.（閱讀是我的嗜好。）某些動詞和所有介系詞後面要接 V-ing：I enjoy cooking.、Thank you for helping me.

A verb + -ing can work as a noun: Reading is my hobby. Some verbs and every preposition are followed by -ing: I enjoy cooking, Thank you for helping me.

注意事項

  • like、love、start 後面接 to V 或 V-ing 都可以：I like swimming. ＝ I like to swim.
    like, love, start take either: I like swimming = I like to swim.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ I enjoy to listen to music.
    ✓ I enjoy listening to music.
    enjoy 後面接 V-ing。
    enjoy takes -ing.
  • ✗ He is good at play tennis.
    ✓ He is good at playing tennis.
    介系詞 at 後面接 V-ing。
    After the preposition at, use -ing.

例句

Have you finished cleaning your room?
你打掃完房間了嗎？
Riding a scooter in the rain is dangerous.
下雨天騎機車很危險。
She's interested in learning Korean.
她對學韓文有興趣。
