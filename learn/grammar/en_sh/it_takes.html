It takes …・spend … V-ing

花費時間和金錢：take、spend、cost

take, spend, cost — English grammar, senior high

接續

It takes ＋ 人 ＋ 時間 ＋ to V・人 ＋ spend ＋ 時間／錢 ＋ V-ing／on ＋ 名詞・東西 ＋ cost ＋ 人 ＋ 錢
It takes + person + time + to verb · person + spend + time / money + verb-ing / on + noun · thing + cost + person + money

說明

三種說「花」的句型主詞不同：It takes me an hour to get to work.（我通勤要一小時。）I spend an hour commuting every day. This bag cost me 2,000 dollars.（這個包包花了我兩千元。）

Three ways to say “spend”, each with a different subject: It takes me an hour to get to work. I spend an hour commuting every day. This bag cost me 2,000 dollars.

注意事項

  • spend 後面接 V-ing 或 on ＋ 名詞，不接 to V。
    spend takes -ing or on + noun, never to + verb.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ I spent two hours to finish the puzzle.
    ✓ I spent two hours finishing the puzzle.
    spend 時間 ＋ V-ing。
    spend time + -ing.
  • ✗ It took me to clean the house three hours.
    ✓ It took me three hours to clean the house.
    順序是 It takes ＋ 人 ＋ 時間 ＋ to V。
    Order: It takes + person + time + to verb.

例句

How long does it take to walk to the station?
走到車站要多久？
She spends too much money on clothes.
她花太多錢買衣服。
The repairs cost us a fortune.
修理花了我們一大筆錢。
