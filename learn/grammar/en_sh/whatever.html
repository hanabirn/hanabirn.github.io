whatever・whoever・wherever

複合關係詞 whatever、whoever

whatever, whoever, wherever — English grammar, senior high

接續

whatever（任何…的東西／不論什麼）・whoever（任何…的人／不論誰）・wherever（不論哪裡）・whenever（不論何時）・however ＋ 形容詞（不論多…）
whatever · whoever · wherever · whenever · however + adjective (no matter how)

說明

這些字可以當「任何…的」：Take whatever you want.（你想拿什麼就拿。）也可以表示讓步「不論…」：Whatever happens, I'll support you.（不管發生什麼，我都支持你。）＝ No matter what happens …

They mean “anything that”: Take whatever you want. They also mean “no matter”: Whatever happens, I'll support you (= No matter what happens …).

注意事項

  • however 後面接形容詞或副詞：However hard it is, don't give up.
    however takes an adjective or adverb: However hard it is, don't give up.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ You can invite whomever wants to come.
    ✓ You can invite whoever wants to come.
    子句裡當主詞（wants 的主詞）用 whoever。
    It's the subject of wants: whoever.
  • ✗ No matter how it is hard, keep going.
    ✓ No matter how hard it is, keep going.
    how 後面緊接形容詞。
    how is followed directly by the adjective.

例句

You can sit wherever you like.
你想坐哪裡都可以。
Whoever finishes first gets a prize.
最先完成的人有獎品。
Call me whenever you need help.
需要幫忙時隨時打給我。
