Never have I …・Not only … but also

倒裝句

Inversion after negative words — English grammar, senior high

接續

否定詞放句首 ＋ 助動詞 ＋ 主詞 ＋ 動詞：Never／Rarely／Seldom／Not only／Only then／No sooner … than
negative word first + auxiliary + subject + verb: Never / Rarely / Seldom / Not only / Only then / No sooner … than

說明

否定或限定的字放在句首加強語氣時，後面要像問句一樣倒裝：I have never seen such a view. → Never have I seen such a view.（我從沒看過這麼美的景色。）Not only is she smart, but she is also kind.

When a negative or limiting word starts the sentence for emphasis, the rest is inverted like a question: Never have I seen such a view. Not only is she smart, but she is also kind.

注意事項

  • 一般動詞要補 do／does／did：Seldom does he eat breakfast.
    Ordinary verbs need do / does / did: Seldom does he eat breakfast.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ Never I have eaten such good food.
    ✓ Never have I eaten such good food.
    Never 在句首要倒裝。
    Never at the start needs inversion.
  • ✗ Rarely he goes to the movies.
    ✓ Rarely does he go to the movies.
    一般動詞倒裝要加 does，動詞回原形。
    Ordinary verbs need does + bare verb.

例句

Not only did he forget my birthday, but he also forgot our dinner.
他不但忘了我的生日，連我們的晚餐約會也忘了。
Seldom do we see such kindness.
我們很少看到這樣的善意。
Only then did I understand what she meant.
直到那時我才明白她的意思。
