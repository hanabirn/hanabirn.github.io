see／hear ＋ O ＋ V／V-ing

感官動詞 see、hear、watch、feel

Verbs of perception — English grammar, senior high

接續

see／hear／watch／feel ＋ 受詞 ＋ 原形（看到整個過程）／V-ing（看到正在進行）
see / hear / watch / feel + object + verb (the whole action) / verb-ing (part of it, in progress)

說明

感官動詞後面的動作可以用原形或 V-ing：I saw him cross the street.（看到他過完馬路，整個過程）I saw him crossing the street.（看到他正在過馬路）兩種都對，意思略有不同。

After perception verbs the action can be a bare verb or -ing: I saw him cross the street (the whole action), I saw him crossing the street (in progress). Both are correct with slightly different meanings.

注意事項

  • 被動時原形要加 to：He was seen to enter the building.
    In the passive the bare verb takes to: He was seen to enter the building.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ I heard someone to call my name.
    ✓ I heard someone call my name.
    感官動詞後面不加 to。
    No to after a perception verb.
  • ✗ We watched the sun to set.
    ✓ We watched the sun set.
    watch 後面接原形或 V-ing。
    watch takes the bare verb or -ing.

例句

I heard the baby crying in the next room.
我聽到寶寶在隔壁房間哭。
Did you see anyone leave the building?
你有看到任何人離開大樓嗎？
She felt the ground shake during the earthquake.
地震時她感覺到地面在搖。
