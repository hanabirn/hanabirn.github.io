…, which …

非限定關係子句（逗號＋which／who）

Non-defining relative clauses — English grammar, senior high

接續

名詞, who／which ＋ 補充說明, …（前後加逗號，不能用 that）・, which 也可以指整句話
noun, who / which + extra information, … (commas; no that) · , which can refer to a whole clause

說明

加逗號的關係子句只是補充說明，拿掉也不影響句意：My sister, who lives in Taipei, is a nurse.（我姊姊住在台北，她是護理師。）, which 也能指前面整句：He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.

With commas, the clause just adds information and could be dropped: My sister, who lives in Taipei, is a nurse. , which can refer to the whole idea: He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.

注意事項

  • 非限定子句不能用 that，也不能省略關係代名詞。
    Non-defining clauses can't use that or drop the pronoun.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ Taipei 101, that was finished in 2004, is very tall.
    ✓ Taipei 101, which was finished in 2004, is very tall.
    逗號後面不能用 that。
    No that after a comma.
  • ✗ She lost her phone, what made her upset.
    ✓ She lost her phone, which made her upset.
    指前面整件事用 , which。
    Referring to the whole clause: , which.

例句

Jiufen, which is near Keelung, is popular with tourists.
九份靠近基隆，很受觀光客歡迎。
My grandmother, who is 90, still walks every day.
我奶奶九十歲了，還是每天散步。
The train was late, which meant we missed the show.
火車誤點，結果我們錯過了表演。
