The ＋ 比較級, the ＋ 比較級

越…就越…

The more …, the more … — English grammar, senior high

接續

The ＋ 比較級 ＋ 主詞 ＋ 動詞, the ＋ 比較級 ＋ 主詞 ＋ 動詞
The + comparative + subject + verb, the + comparative + subject + verb

說明

說兩件事一起變化：The more you practice, the better you get.（你練得越多，就會越好。）The sooner, the better.（越快越好。）比較級放在 the 後面，句首要有 the。

For two things that change together: The more you practice, the better you get. The sooner, the better. Each half starts with the + comparative.

注意事項

  • 名詞也可以：The more money he makes, the more he spends.
    Nouns work too: The more money he makes, the more he spends.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ More you read, more you learn.
    ✓ The more you read, the more you learn.
    兩邊都要有 the。
    Both halves need the.
  • ✗ The harder you work, the more successful you will be more.
    ✓ The harder you work, the more successful you will be.
    比較級只放在 the 後面一次。
    The comparative appears once, after the.

例句

The older I get, the more I appreciate my parents.
我年紀越大，越感謝我的爸媽。
The earlier we leave, the less traffic there will be.
我們越早出發，車就越少。
The more, the merrier!
人越多越熱鬧！
