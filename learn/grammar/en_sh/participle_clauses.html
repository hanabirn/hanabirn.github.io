V-ing …, ／ p.p. …,

分詞構句

Participle clauses — English grammar, senior high

接續

主動：V-ing …, 主句（Walking home, I met Amy.）・被動：p.p. …, 主句（Written in 1900, the book …）・先發生：Having ＋ p.p. …,
active: V-ing …, main clause · passive: p.p. …, main clause · earlier action: Having + p.p. …,

說明

兩個子句主詞相同時，可以把一個子句縮成分詞：When I walked home, I met Amy. → Walking home, I met Amy. 主詞「被」做用過去分詞：Built in 1920, the station is a historic site.

When both clauses share a subject, one can become a participle: When I walked home, I met Amy → Walking home, I met Amy. If the subject is acted on, use the past participle: Built in 1920, the station is a historic site.

注意事項

  • 分詞的主詞必須跟主句主詞一樣，不然會變成「懸垂分詞」的錯誤。
    The participle's subject must be the main clause's subject, or it “dangles”.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ Walking into the room, the lights went off.
    ✓ Walking into the room, I saw the lights go off.
    走進房間的是「我」，不是燈。
    I walked in, not the lights.
  • ✗ Writing in simple English, the book is easy to read.
    ✓ Written in simple English, the book is easy to read.
    書是「被寫」，用過去分詞。
    The book was written: past participle.

例句

Feeling tired, she went to bed early.
她覺得累，就早早睡了。
Having finished his homework, Tom went out to play.
湯姆寫完功課後就出去玩了。
Surrounded by mountains, the village is very quiet.
那個村子四面環山，非常安靜。
