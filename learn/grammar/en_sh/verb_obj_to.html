ask／tell／want ＋ sb ＋ to V

動詞 ＋ 人 ＋ to V

Verb + person + to-verb — English grammar, senior high

接續

ask／tell／want／expect／allow／encourage／remind／advise ＋ 受詞 ＋ (not) to ＋ 原形
ask / tell / want / expect / allow / encourage / remind / advise + object + (not) to + verb

說明

這些動詞後面先接「人」，再接 to ＋ 原形，表示要那個人做的事：My parents want me to be a doctor.（我爸媽希望我當醫生。）The doctor advised him to rest.

These verbs take a person, then to + verb for what that person should do: My parents want me to be a doctor. The doctor advised him to rest.

注意事項

  • 否定放在 to 前面：She reminded me not to be late.
    The negative goes before to: She reminded me not to be late.
  • suggest 不能用這個句型：I suggest that he (should) rest.
    suggest doesn't fit this pattern: I suggest that he (should) rest.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ I want that you come to my party.
    ✓ I want you to come to my party.
    want ＋ 人 ＋ to V。
    want + person + to verb.
  • ✗ She told me don't worry.
    ✓ She told me not to worry.
    否定是 not to ＋ 原形。
    The negative is not to + verb.

例句

The coach encouraged us to keep trying.
教練鼓勵我們繼續努力。
Could you remind me to buy milk?
你可以提醒我買牛奶嗎？
My school doesn't allow students to dye their hair.
我們學校不准學生染頭髮。
