where・when・why

關係副詞 where、when、why

Relative adverbs: where, when, why — English grammar, senior high

接續

地方 ＋ where ＋ 子句・時間 ＋ when ＋ 子句・the reason ＋ why ＋ 子句
place + where + clause · time + when + clause · the reason + why + clause

說明

說明地方、時間、原因時用關係副詞：This is the restaurant where we first met.（這是我們第一次見面的餐廳。）I'll never forget the day when I got my results. That's the reason why I quit.

Use relative adverbs for places, times and reasons: This is the restaurant where we first met. I'll never forget the day when I got my results. That's the reason why I quit.

注意事項

  • where ＝ in／at which，when ＝ on／in which。
    where = in / at which; when = on / in which.
  • 子句缺受詞時用 which／that，不用 where：the city (that) I visited。
    If the clause lacks an object, use which / that, not where: the city (that) I visited.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ This is the park where I like.
    ✓ This is the park (that) I like.
    like 缺受詞，要用 that／which。
    like needs an object: use that / which.
  • ✗ I remember the year which we moved here.
    ✓ I remember the year when we moved here.
    子句完整、說時間，用 when。
    The clause is complete and it's a time: when.

例句

Tainan is the city where I was born.
台南是我出生的城市。
Summer is the season when we go camping.
夏天是我們去露營的季節。
Nobody knows the reason why he left.
沒有人知道他離開的原因。
