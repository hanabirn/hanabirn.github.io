make／let／have ＋ O ＋ V

使役動詞 make、let、have、get

Causatives: make, let, have, get — English grammar, senior high

接續

make／let／have ＋ 受詞 ＋ 原形・get ＋ 受詞 ＋ to V・have／get ＋ 東西 ＋ p.p.（請人把東西…）
make / let / have + object + verb · get + object + to verb · have / get + thing + p.p. (have something done)

說明

make 是「迫使」，let 是「讓、允許」，have 是「叫（某人做）」，後面都接原形：My mom made me clean my room. Let me help you. 請別人幫你處理東西用 have ＋ 東西 ＋ p.p.：I had my hair cut.（我去剪了頭髮。）

make means force, let means allow, have means arrange for someone to do it — all with the bare verb: My mom made me clean my room. Let me help you. For services: have + thing + p.p.: I had my hair cut.

注意事項

  • 被動時 make 要加 to：I was made to wait.
    In the passive, make takes to: I was made to wait.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ My boss made me to work late.
    ✓ My boss made me work late.
    make 後面接原形。
    make takes the bare verb.
  • ✗ I cut my hair at the salon yesterday.
    ✓ I had my hair cut at the salon yesterday.
    請別人剪頭髮用 have ＋ hair ＋ cut。
    Someone else cut it: have my hair cut.

例句

The sad movie made everyone cry.
那部悲傷的電影讓大家都哭了。
My parents let me stay up late on Fridays.
我爸媽星期五讓我晚睡。
We're having our kitchen repainted.
我們請人重新粉刷廚房。
