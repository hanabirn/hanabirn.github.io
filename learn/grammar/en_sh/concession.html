文法 › 英文 高中

although・despite・in spite of

讓步：although、despite、in spite of

Contrast: although, despite, in spite of — English grammar, senior high

接續

although／though／even though ＋ 子句・despite／in spite of ＋ 名詞／V-ing
although / though / even though + clause · despite / in spite of + noun / verb-ing

說明

although 後面接完整的句子：Although it was raining, we went out. despite／in spite of 後面接名詞或 V-ing：Despite the rain, we went out. In spite of feeling sick, she went to school.

although takes a full clause: Although it was raining, we went out. despite / in spite of take a noun or -ing: Despite the rain, we went out. In spite of feeling sick, she went to school.

注意事項

  • despite 後面沒有 of；in spite of 要有 of。
    despite has no of; in spite of does.
  • although 和 but 不一起用。
    Don't use although and but together.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ Despite it was late, he kept working.
    ✓ Although it was late, he kept working.
    後面是完整句子要用 although。
    A full clause needs although.
  • ✗ Despite of the traffic, we arrived on time.
    ✓ Despite the traffic, we arrived on time.
    despite 不加 of。
    despite has no of.

例句

Even though he's rich, he lives a simple life.
他雖然有錢，卻過著簡樸的生活。
Despite the high price, the tickets sold out quickly.
儘管票價很高，票還是很快就賣完了。
In spite of being tired, she finished the race.
儘管很累，她還是跑完了全程。
