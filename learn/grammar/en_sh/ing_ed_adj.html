-ing ／ -ed 形容詞

分詞形容詞：interesting 和 interested

-ing and -ed adjectives — English grammar, senior high

接續

-ing：讓人有某種感覺的人事物（boring 令人無聊的）・-ed：人自己的感覺（bored 覺得無聊）
-ing: what causes the feeling (boring) · -ed: how the person feels (bored)

說明

-ing 形容詞描述「讓人覺得…的東西」，-ed 描述「人感到…」：The movie was boring, so I was bored.（電影很無聊，所以我覺得無聊。）I'm interested in history because it's interesting.

-ing adjectives describe what causes a feeling; -ed adjectives describe how people feel: The movie was boring, so I was bored. I'm interested in history because it's interesting.

注意事項

  • 常見的一組：exciting／excited、tiring／tired、surprising／surprised、confusing／confused。
    Common pairs: exciting / excited, tiring / tired, surprising / surprised, confusing / confused.

常見錯誤

  • ✗ I'm very boring in math class.
    ✓ I'm very bored in math class.
    說自己的感覺用 bored（I'm boring 是「我很無趣」）。
    Your feeling is bored (I'm boring means you're dull).
  • ✗ The news was very surprised.
    ✓ The news was very surprising.
    讓人驚訝的消息用 surprising。
    News that surprises is surprising.

例句

The trip was tiring, but we were very excited.
旅程很累，但我們很興奮。
I was confused by the confusing instructions.
說明寫得很混亂，讓我看得一頭霧水。
Are you interested in joining the club?
你有興趣加入社團嗎？
