There are more clients this time; Xiao Chen will go with you.

I chat with Taiwanese friends every day and have watched lots of Taiwanese TV dramas.

I've finished this novel — the story is really interesting.

Can I borrow it? I'll give it back next month.

OK, I'll move it right away.
第 8 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女先生，對不起，這裡不能拍照。
Sorry, sir, no photos in here.
男對不起，我不知道。那哪裡可以拍？
Sorry, I didn't know. Where can I take photos, then?
女外面的花園可以，裡面都不行。
In the garden outside, yes; nowhere inside.
男好的，謝謝。
OK, thanks.
第 9 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）

女去了一趟墾丁，可是下了兩天雨。
Kenting — but it rained for two days.
男那太可惜了。
What a shame.
女對啊，只好在飯店裡看電視。
Yes, we just watched TV in the hotel.
第 10 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女你好，我想把這些書寄到日本。
Hello, I'd like to send these books to Japan.
男好，請先放在這裡，我看看多重。
Sure, put them here and I'll weigh them.
女大概要幾天才會到？
About how many days will it take?
男一般一個星期左右。
Usually about a week.
第 11 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男你看起來很累，昨天沒睡好嗎？
You look tired — didn't you sleep well?
女我鄰居家的狗叫了一個晚上。
My neighbour's dog barked all night.
男那你要不要去跟鄰居說一下？
Maybe you should have a word with them?
女我打算今天晚上去找他們。
I'm going to see them this evening.

逐字稿（聽完再看）

This restaurant is packed — how long do we have to wait?
男服務員說大概要半個小時。
The waiter says about half an hour.
女太久了，我們去吃別家吧。
Too long — let's eat somewhere else.
男好，前面還有一家麵店。
OK, there's a noodle shop just ahead.
第 13 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）

Mum, next month I want to go and work in Australia.
女一個人去？你英文還可以嗎？
On your own? Is your English good enough?
男沒問題，我學了很多年了。
No problem — I've studied it for years.
女那你到了以後要常打電話回家。
Then call home often once you're there.

逐字稿（聽完再看）
女你們公司明天放假嗎？
Is your company closed tomorrow?
男明天大家都放假，可是我們公司還是要上班。
Tomorrow is a holiday for everyone, but our company still works.
女那太辛苦了。
That's tough.
男還好，下個星期可以多休息一天。
It's fine — we get an extra day off next week.

Ladies and gentlemen, welcome aboard. We take off in twenty minutes; please switch off your phones and stay seated.
第 16 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女明天會下很大的雨，學校已經通知明天不上課了。大家在家要把窗戶關好，不要出門，有事就打電話給老師。
There will be very heavy rain tomorrow and the school has announced there are no classes. At home, shut the windows and stay in; call the teacher if you need anything.
第 17 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男我們學校的圖書館平常早上八點開門，晚上十點關門。考試前兩個星期，會開到晚上十二點。
Our school library normally opens at eight and closes at ten at night. In the two weeks before exams it stays open until midnight.
第 18 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女這家店每個月的第一個星期天休息，其他時間都從早上十點開到晚上九點。
This shop is closed on the first Sunday of every month; otherwise it's open from ten in the morning to nine at night.
第 19 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男歡迎大家參加今天的活動。活動有兩個部分：上午參觀茶園，下午大家一起學做茶。中午我們在山上的餐廳吃飯。
Welcome to today's event. There are two parts: in the morning we visit a tea garden, in the afternoon we learn to make tea together. We'll have lunch at a restaurant on the mountain.
第 20 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女我在一家咖啡店打工，已經做了半年了。工作不太難，可是要記的東西很多，比如怎麼收錢、怎麼包東西、怎麼做咖啡。剛開始我常常出錯，現在已經都習慣了。店長說，如果我做得好，下個月可以多給我一點錢。
I've worked part-time at a café for six months. The work isn't hard, but there's a lot to remember — taking money, wrapping things, making coffee. At first I often made mistakes; now I'm used to it all. The manager says if I do well, I can get a bit more money next month.
第 21 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男我爺爺今年八十歲了，以前是一名中學老師。他很喜歡學新東西，去年還學會了用手機上網。現在他每天都在網上看新聞，還常常跟住在國外的孫子視訊聊天。
My grandfather is eighty this year; he used to be a secondary-school teacher. He loves learning new things — last year he learnt to use the internet on his phone. Now he reads the news online every day and often video-chats with his grandson abroad.
第 22 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女各位同學，這個星期六我們要去陽明山爬山。早上七點半在學校門口見，大家一起坐車去。山上比較冷，請多帶一件衣服。中午我們在山上吃飯，下午四點左右回到學校。
Everyone, this Saturday we're going hiking on Yangmingshan. Meet at the school gate at 7:30 and we'll go together by coach. It's colder on the mountain, so bring an extra layer. We'll have lunch up there and be back at school around four.
第 23 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男很多人早上來不及吃早飯，常常喝一杯咖啡就去上班了。醫生說，不吃早飯對身體很不好，上午工作的時候也比較累。其實，前一天晚上先準備好簡單的早飯，早上只要五分鐘就能吃完。
Many people have no time for breakfast and just drink a coffee on the way to work. Doctors say skipping breakfast is bad for you and leaves you more tired at work in the morning. In fact, if you prepare something simple the night before, breakfast takes only five minutes.
第 24 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女我叫林小芳，以前在銀行工作，每天都要加班。三年前，我決定回到花蓮老家，開了一家小飯館。雖然收入沒有以前多，但是每天都能看到山和海，我覺得很幸福。
My name is Lin Xiaofang. I used to work at a bank and did overtime every day. Three years ago I decided to go back to my hometown, Hualien, and opened a small restaurant. I earn less than before, but I see the mountains and the sea every day, and I feel very happy.
第 25 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男現在很多年輕人喜歡在網上點外送。只要在手機上點幾下，半個小時飯就送到了。點外送確實很方便，可是外送的飯菜常常比較油，也比較鹹，常吃對身體不好。而且，外送用了很多用一次就丟的盒子，也會有很多垃圾。所以，有時間的話，還是自己做飯比較好。
Many young people now order food delivery online: a few taps on your phone and it arrives in half an hour. It's certainly convenient, but delivery food is often oily and salty, and eating it a lot is bad for you. It also uses lots of throwaway boxes, which means a lot of rubbish. So if you have time, it's better to cook for yourself.
