Chinese listening practice, HSK 4, round 3 — 25 recordings, 32 questions

每一段都是母語錄音。建議先到網站聽、作答，再回來看逐字稿。

Why are you finishing so late?

Clients came today; the meeting ran until seven.

Sure, but on Saturday I'm taking my mum to hospital.

There seems to be an accident ahead.

逐字稿（聽完再看）

Your Chinese is so good — how long have you studied?
男在台灣學了兩年，回國以後又自己學了一年。
Two years in Taiwan and another year on my own back home.

逐字稿（聽完再看）

男昨天做飯的時候不小心被刀切到了。
I cut it with a knife while cooking yesterday.
第 7 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男你要不要一起去吃火鍋？
Want to come for hot pot?
女不了，我最近在減肥，晚上只吃青菜。
No — I'm on a diet and only eat greens in the evening.
第 8 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女請問，去故宮要坐幾號公車？
Excuse me, which bus goes to the Palace Museum?
男你可以坐紅三十號，在前面的站牌等。
Take the Red 30 — wait at the stop ahead.
女大概要坐多久？
About how long?
男不塞車的話，二十分鐘就到了。
Twenty minutes if there's no traffic.
第 9 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男你怎麼想到要開咖啡店？
How did you come to open a café?
女我以前在銀行工作，壓力太大了。
I used to work at a bank — too much stress.
男那現在呢？
And now?
女雖然很累，但是每天都很開心。
It's tiring, but I'm happy every day.
第 10 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女這間房子的房租一個月多少？
How much is the rent here a month?
男一萬五，包括水電。
Fifteen thousand, including utilities.
女有點貴，可以便宜一點嗎？
A bit expensive — can it be lower?
男如果你租兩年，可以算你一萬四。
If you rent for two years, fourteen thousand.
第 11 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男你昨天怎麼沒來參加婚禮？
Why didn't you come to the wedding yesterday?
女我發燒了，在家躺了一天。
I had a fever and stayed in bed all day.
男現在好點了嗎？
Are you better now?
女好多了，謝謝。幫我跟新娘說聲恭喜。
Much better, thanks. Please congratulate the bride for me.
第 12 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女你們學校的運動會什麼時候舉行？
When is your school's sports day?
男本來是這個星期五。
It was going to be this Friday.
女本來？
Going to be?
男因為會下雨，改到下個星期了。
It'll rain, so it's been moved to next week.
第 13 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）

Can I borrow this book when you've finished?
女可以啊，不過這是圖書館的書。
Sure, but it's a library book.
男那什麼時候要還？
When is it due?
女下個星期一，你要在那之前還喔。
Next Monday — you need to return it before then.
第 14 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）

Why did you cycle to work today?
男我的機車壞了，送去修了。
My scooter broke down; it's being repaired.
女騎了多久？
How long did it take?
男四十分鐘，有點累，但是還不錯。
Forty minutes — a bit tiring but not bad.
第 15 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男我們這裡的老人越來越多，所以社區開了一個老人活動中心，每天都有唱歌、下棋等活動。
There are more and more older people here, so the community opened a seniors' centre with singing, chess and more every day.
第 16 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）

Every time I go to Hualien I buy mochi; it's soft and not too sweet, and my family love it.
第 17 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）

Staff at this company can choose their own hours, as long as they work eight hours a day.
第 18 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女最近很多年輕人喜歡去山上露營，他們說晚上在山上看星星，可以忘掉城市的壓力。
Lately many young people like camping in the mountains; they say watching the stars helps them forget city stress.
第 19 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男這次的考試比上次難多了，不過我們班的平均分數反而比上次高，老師很高興。
This exam was much harder than the last, but our class average was actually higher, and the teacher was pleased.
第 20 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女我在一家旅行社工作，最忙的時候是寒暑假。很多家庭會在這個時候出國，所以我們要提早幾個月安排機票和飯店。雖然很累，但是看到客人玩得開心，我也很有成就感。
I work at a travel agency; the busiest times are the winter and summer holidays, when many families go abroad, so we book flights and hotels months ahead. It's tiring, but seeing customers enjoy themselves gives me a sense of achievement.
第 21 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男各位同學，下個星期三學校要舉辦一場演講比賽，題目是「我的家鄉」。想參加的同學請在這個星期五以前到辦公室報名，每個人可以講五分鐘。
Students, next Wednesday the school is holding a speech contest on 'My Home Town'. Anyone who wants to take part should sign up at the office by this Friday; each speaker has five minutes.
第 22 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女很多人喜歡在網路上買東西，因為又方便又便宜。不過我比較喜歡去商店買，因為可以先看看、試試，也不用擔心收到的東西跟照片不一樣。
Many people like shopping online because it's easy and cheap. I prefer shops, though: I can look and try first, and needn't worry that what arrives looks different from the photo.
第 23 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男我爺爺今年八十歲了，每天早上五點就起床，先去公園打太極拳，然後回家看報紙。他說身體好的祕密就是早睡早起、多動一動。
My grandpa is eighty; he gets up at five every morning, does tai chi in the park, then reads the paper at home. He says his secret is early to bed, early to rise, and keeping active.
第 24 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女我們學校最近開始讓學生帶自己的水壺，不再賣瓶裝水。一開始有些同學覺得不方便，可是一個學期下來，學校的垃圾少了很多。
Our school has started asking pupils to bring their own bottles and no longer sells bottled water. At first some found it inconvenient, but after a term there's much less rubbish.
第 25 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女台灣有很多夜市，每個夜市都有自己的特色。像台北的士林夜市很大，東西很多；台南的花園夜市有很多傳統小吃；基隆的廟口夜市則以海鮮出名。很多外國遊客來台灣，一定會去夜市走走，有人甚至一天去兩個。
Taiwan has many night markets, each with its own character: Taipei's Shilin is big with lots to buy; Tainan's Garden market has many traditional snacks; Keelung's Miaokou is famous for seafood. Foreign visitors always visit one — some even go to two a day.
