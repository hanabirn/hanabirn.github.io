第 9 段　請聽一段學生和輔導員的對話。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白Listen to a conversation between a student and an adviser.
請聽一段學生和輔導員的對話。
女I'm thinking about studying abroad next year, but I'm worried it will delay my graduation.
我在考慮明年出國交換，但擔心會延畢。
男Not necessarily. Many of our partner universities offer courses that count toward your major.
不一定。我們很多姊妹校的課可以抵主修學分。
女Really? How do I find out which ones?
真的嗎？要怎麼知道是哪些？
男Check the study-abroad website, then bring me a list of courses you're interested in. I'll confirm them with your department.
先看出國交換的網站，然後把有興趣的課列給我，我會跟你們系上確認。
第 10 段　請聽一段兩個學生的對話。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白Listen to a conversation between two students.
請聽一段兩個學生的對話。
男Did you hear the campus gym is closing for renovation next month?
妳聽說學校健身房下個月要整修關閉嗎？
女No! Where are we supposed to work out?
沒有！那我們要去哪運動？
男The university made a deal with a gym downtown. Students can use it for free if they show their ID.
學校跟市區一家健身房合作，學生出示學生證就能免費使用。
女That's good, but it's a twenty-minute bus ride. I think I'll just run in the park instead.
不錯，但要搭二十分鐘公車。我想我乾脆去公園跑步。

第 14 段　請聽一段藝術史課的講課。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白Listen to part of a lecture in an art history class.
請聽一段藝術史課的講課。
男When we look at old paintings, we often see colors that seem dull. But many of them were once much brighter. Some pigments used by painters, like certain yellows and reds, fade when exposed to light over centuries. Others change color because of chemical reactions with the air. Today, museums protect paintings by keeping the lights low and controlling the humidity. Scientists can even use X-rays to discover the original colors hidden underneath.
我們看古畫時，常常覺得顏色很暗淡。但很多畫以前鮮豔得多。畫家用的某些顏料，例如某些黃色和紅色，經過幾世紀照光就會褪色；另一些則因為和空氣起化學反應而變色。現在博物館用低亮度的燈光和控制濕度來保護畫作。科學家甚至能用 X 光找出底下原本的顏色。
第 15 段　請聽一段商業課的講課。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白Listen to part of a lecture in a business class.
請聽一段商業課的講課。
女Let's discuss why some small businesses succeed where large companies fail. Take local bookstores. Ten years ago, many people predicted online retailers would close them all. Instead, a lot of independent bookstores are doing well. Why? They offer something online shops can't: personal recommendations from staff, author events and a sense of community. In other words, they stopped competing on price and started competing on experience.
我們來討論為什麼有些小商家能在大公司失敗的地方成功。以在地書店為例。十年前很多人預測網路零售商會讓書店全部倒閉。結果很多獨立書店經營得很好。為什麼？它們提供網路商店做不到的東西：店員的個人推薦、作家活動和社群感。換句話說，它們不再拚價格，而是拚體驗。
