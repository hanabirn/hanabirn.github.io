第 14 段　請聽一段環境科學課的講課。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白Listen to part of a lecture in an environmental science class.
請聽一段環境科學課的講課。
女Let's talk about urban heat islands. Cities are often several degrees warmer than the countryside around them. Why? Dark surfaces such as asphalt and roofs absorb sunlight during the day and release that heat slowly at night. Cities also have fewer trees, and trees cool the air by releasing water from their leaves. Some cities are fighting back by painting roofs white and planting rooftop gardens. In one study, a white roof stayed about thirty degrees cooler than a dark one on a summer afternoon.
我們來談城市熱島。城市常常比周圍的鄉村熱好幾度。為什麼？柏油路和屋頂這類深色表面白天吸收陽光，晚上慢慢把熱放出來。城市的樹也比較少，而樹會透過葉子釋放水分讓空氣變涼。有些城市開始反擊，把屋頂漆成白色、種屋頂花園。一項研究中，夏天下午白色屋頂比深色屋頂涼了大約三十度。
第 15 段　請聽一段心理學課的講課。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白Listen to part of a lecture in a psychology class.
請聽一段心理學課的講課。
男Today's topic is memory and sleep. For a long time, people thought sleep was simply rest for the brain. We now know the brain is very active during sleep. In one experiment, students learned a list of words, then half of them slept and half stayed awake. The next day, those who slept remembered far more. Researchers believe that during deep sleep the brain replays what we learned and moves it into long-term memory. So staying up all night before an exam may actually hurt your score.
今天的主題是記憶與睡眠。長久以來大家以為睡覺只是讓大腦休息。現在我們知道睡眠時大腦非常活躍。一項實驗中，學生學一串單字，之後一半睡覺、一半熬夜。隔天睡覺的那組記得多得多。研究者認為深層睡眠時，大腦會重播學過的東西，把它轉進長期記憶。所以考前熬夜其實可能讓分數變差。
