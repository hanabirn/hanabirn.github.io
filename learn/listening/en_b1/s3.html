英文 B1 聽力練習 第 3 回

English listening practice, B1, round 3 — 15 recordings, 25 questions

每一段都是母語錄音。建議先到網站聽、作答，再回來看逐字稿。

內容

第 1 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男What are you having for lunch?
你午餐要吃什麼？
女I'd love some pasta, but it takes too long, and the soup's finished. I'll just have a cheese sandwich.
我很想吃義大利麵，可是要煮太久，湯也賣完了。我就吃個起司三明治吧。
第 2 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女Is your sister still learning the guitar?
你妹妹還在學吉他嗎？
男She gave up — her fingers hurt too much. Now she sings in a band. She wanted to play the drums, but our neighbours would complain.
她放棄了，手指太痛。現在她在一個樂團唱歌。她想打鼓，可是鄰居會抱怨。
第 3 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男What are you taking on holiday? Your surfboard?
你度假要帶什麼？衝浪板嗎？
女No, we're going to the mountains. There's loads of snow this year, so I'm taking my skis. I'll leave my bike at home.
不是，我們要去山上。今年雪很多，所以我帶滑雪板。腳踏車就留在家。
第 4 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女Did you cycle to work today?
你今天騎腳踏車上班嗎？
男No, my bike's got a flat tyre, and my scooter's at my brother's. So I drove — and sat in traffic for an hour!
沒有，我的腳踏車爆胎了，機車又在我哥那裡。所以我開車，結果塞了一個小時！
第 5 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男Where's the cat? She's not on the bed.
貓呢？她不在床上。
女She's not under the table either. Oh look — she's on the windowsill, watching the birds.
也不在桌子底下。啊，你看，她在窗台上看鳥。
第 6 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女Did you get a new laptop for university?
你為了上大學買新筆電了嗎？
男I was going to, but they're so expensive. I've bought a tablet with a keyboard — it's lighter, too. My phone's too small for writing essays.
本來要買，可是筆電好貴。我買了一台附鍵盤的平板，也比較輕。手機太小，不能寫報告。
第 7 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男What did your dad do at the weekend?
你爸爸週末做了什麼？
女Mum asked him to cut the grass, but it rained all weekend, so he stayed in and painted the kitchen. The car still needs fixing!
媽媽要他割草，可是整個週末都在下雨，所以他待在家裡油漆廚房。車子還是需要修！
第 8 段　你會聽到一個男孩談一場足球賽。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白You will hear a boy talking about a football match.
你會聽到一個男孩談一場足球賽。
男We lost three-nil, but honestly, the other team were just better than us. Our coach said we played well, and I agree.
我們零比三輸了，不過老實說，對方就是比我們強。教練說我們踢得不錯，我也同意。
第 9 段　你會聽到兩個朋友談一支新手機。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白You will hear two friends talking about a new phone.
你會聽到兩個朋友談一支新手機。
女Is your new phone any good?
你的新手機好用嗎？
男The camera's brilliant, but the battery doesn't last long. I have to charge it twice a day.
相機超棒，可是電池撐不久，我一天要充兩次電。
第 10 段　你會聽到一位女士在火車站。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白You will hear a woman at a train station.
你會聽到一位女士在火車站。
女Excuse me, is this the train to Oxford?
不好意思，這是去牛津的火車嗎？
男It goes to Oxford, but it stops at every station, so it is very slow. The fast train leaves in ten minutes from platform three.
是會到牛津，可是每站都停，很慢。快車十分鐘後從第三月台發車。
第 11 段　你會聽到一個女孩談當志工。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白You will hear a girl talking about volunteering.
你會聽到一個女孩談當志工。
女I help at the animal shelter every Saturday. The dogs need walking and the cages need cleaning, but my favourite job is feeding the cats.
我每個星期六在動物收容所幫忙。狗要遛，籠子要清，不過我最喜歡的工作是餵貓。
第 12 段　你會聽到一位男士和鄰居說話。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白You will hear a man talking to his neighbour.
你會聽到一位男士和鄰居說話。
男Could you look after my plants while I'm away next week? Just water them every two days. I'll leave the key under the mat.
我下週不在，可以幫我照顧植物嗎？每兩天澆一次水就好。我會把鑰匙放在腳踏墊底下。
第 13 段　你會聽到兩個學生談一場考試。
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白You will hear two students talking about an exam.
你會聽到兩個學生談一場考試。
女How did the maths exam go?
數學考得怎麼樣？
男Better than I expected. I didn't finish the last question, but I think I got everything else right.
比我預期的好。最後一題沒寫完，不過其他的我應該都對了。
第 14 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男And now some local news. From next Monday, the city's new bike-hire scheme opens to the public. There will be three hundred bikes at forty stations around the city centre. To use the bikes, you'll first need to download the City Bikes app. The first thirty minutes of every ride are free, and after that it costs one pound for each hour. The bikes are electric, so they're easy to ride up the hills on the north side of the city. Riders must be at least sixteen years old. Helmets aren't provided, so please bring your own. The council hopes the scheme will reduce traffic, and if it's popular, more stations will open next spring.
接下來是地方新聞。從下週一開始，市區新的租借單車計畫開放民眾使用。市中心四十個站點共有三百輛單車。要使用單車，得先下載「城市單車」App。每次騎乘前三十分鐘免費，之後每小時一英鎊。單車是電動的，騎上城北的山坡也很輕鬆。騎乘者至少要十六歲。不提供安全帽，請自備。市議會希望這個計畫能減少車流，如果受歡迎，明年春天會再開更多站點。
第 15 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男Today I'm talking to Nina Shah, who started a repair café in her town two years ago. Nina, what exactly is a repair café?
今天我要訪問妮娜·夏，她兩年前在她住的小鎮開了一間「修理咖啡館」。妮娜，修理咖啡館到底是什麼？
女It's a free event where people bring broken things — lamps, toasters, clothes, bikes — and volunteers help them fix them. The idea is that you learn to repair things yourself, not just watch.
是一個免費的活動，大家帶壞掉的東西來，像檯燈、烤麵包機、衣服、腳踏車，志工幫他們修。重點是你自己學會修，不是只在旁邊看。
男How did it start?
是怎麼開始的？
女My laptop broke while I was at university, and the shop said it would be cheaper to buy a new one. My uncle, who's an engineer, fixed it in twenty minutes. I thought: how many things do we throw away that only need a small repair?
我讀大學時筆電壞了，店家說買新的還比較便宜。我舅舅是工程師，二十分鐘就修好了。我心想：我們丟掉了多少只要小修一下的東西？
男Who are the volunteers?
志工是哪些人？
女Mostly retired people with practical skills — a former electrician, a tailor, a bike mechanic. They love sharing what they know.
大多是有實用技能的退休人士，像以前的電工、裁縫、腳踏車技師。他們很喜歡分享所學。
男What's the most unusual thing anyone has brought?
有人帶來過最特別的東西是什麼？
女A hundred-year-old clock that belonged to a man's great-grandmother. It took three months, but it works again now.
一個一百年的老時鐘，是一位男士曾祖母的。花了三個月，不過現在又能走了。
男What's your plan for the future?
你未來有什麼計畫？
女At the moment we meet once a month in the town hall. I'd like to have a permanent workshop that's open every weekend.
目前我們每個月在鎮公所聚一次。我希望有一間固定的工作坊，每個週末都開。
