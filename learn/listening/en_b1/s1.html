第 15 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女My guest today is Leo Grant, who at just twenty-three runs his own restaurant. Leo, when did you first get interested in cooking?
今天的來賓是李歐·葛蘭特，他才二十三歲就經營自己的餐廳。李歐，你什麼時候開始對做菜有興趣的？
男When I was about ten. My mum worked late, so I often made dinner for my younger brothers. At first it was just pasta, but I started experimenting.
大概十歲的時候。我媽媽工作到很晚，所以我常幫弟弟們做晚餐。一開始只會煮義大利麵，後來開始嘗試各種做法。
女Did you go to cooking school?
你讀過廚藝學校嗎？
男No. I started a business course at university, but I left after a year and got a job in a hotel kitchen. I learnt far more there than I would have in a classroom.
沒有。我在大學讀過商科，但讀了一年就休學，到飯店廚房工作。我在那裡學到的比在教室裡多得多。
女What's the hardest thing about running a restaurant?
經營餐廳最難的是什麼？
男Finding good staff, definitely. The cooking is the easy part. And the hours are very long — I rarely get home before midnight.
一定是找到好員工。做菜反而是簡單的部分。還有工時很長，我很少半夜前到家。
女What's next for you?
你接下來有什麼計畫？
男I'd like to write a cookbook for young people who are learning to cook for themselves, like I did.
我想寫一本食譜，給像我當年那樣、正在學自己做飯的年輕人。
