第 15 段　In recent years running at night has become ever more popular with young people. Many work during the day with no time to exercise, so they use the evening to run. Compared with daytime, it's cooler at night with fewer cars and people, so running is more comfortable. But there are things to watch: wear bright clothes so drivers can see you; choose lit roads where possible; and don't eat or go to bed straight after running.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白这几年，夜跑在年轻人中越来越流行。很多人白天要上班，没有时间运动，只好利用晚上的时间去跑步。和白天相比，晚上天气凉快，路上的车和人也少，跑起来更舒服。不过，夜跑也有需要注意的地方：最好穿颜色亮一点儿的衣服，让司机容易看见；尽量选择有灯的路；跑完以后不要马上吃东西或者睡觉。
In recent years running at night has become ever more popular with young people. Many work during the day with no time to exercise, so they use the evening to run. Compared with daytime, it's cooler at night with fewer cars and people, so running is more comfortable. But there are things to watch: wear bright clothes so drivers can see you; choose lit roads where possible; and don't eat or go to bed straight after running.
第 16 段　Xiao Chen goes to work by metro every day. One morning it poured, and at the station entrance she saw an old woman without an umbrella standing there, not knowing what to do. Xiao Chen gave her own umbrella to the old woman and ran to the office, getting soaked. A week later a new manager joined the company — and he turned out to be that old woman's son. Back home, she had kept telling him about it. The new manager said to Xiao Chen, "Thank you for helping my mother that day."
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白小陈每天坐地铁上班。有一天早上下大雨，她在地铁站门口看到一位老奶奶没带伞，站在那里不知道怎么办。小陈就把自己的伞给了老奶奶，自己跑着去了公司，衣服全湿了。一个星期以后，公司来了一位新经理，原来他就是那位老奶奶的儿子。老奶奶回家以后，一直跟儿子说起这件事。新经理对小陈说：“谢谢你那天帮了我妈妈。”
Xiao Chen goes to work by metro every day. One morning it poured, and at the station entrance she saw an old woman without an umbrella standing there, not knowing what to do. Xiao Chen gave her own umbrella to the old woman and ran to the office, getting soaked. A week later a new manager joined the company — and he turned out to be that old woman's son. 第 17 段　There are many Chinese surnames, but only a few are really common. According to surveys, Wang, Li, Zhang, Liu and Chen are the most common; together those five account for nearly a third of the population. Chinese people put the surname before the given name, and children mostly take their father's surname. But now more and more children take their mother's, and some have both parents' surnames in their names.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白中国人的姓很多，但是最常见的只有几个。根据调查，姓王、李、张、刘、陈的人最多，这五个姓的人加起来，差不多占了全国人口的三分之一。中国人一般把姓放在名字的前面，而且孩子大多跟爸爸姓。不过，现在也有越来越多的孩子跟妈妈姓，还有的孩子的名字里，同时有爸爸和妈妈的姓。

Chinese people put the surname before the given name, and children mostly take their father's surname. But now more and more children take their mother's, and some have both parents' surnames in their names.
第 18 段　Did you know? A cat sleeps twelve to sixteen hours a day — about two-thirds of its day. Cats sleep so much because, living outdoors, they depend on catching small animals, which takes a lot of energy, so they sleep to save their strength. Also, although cats sleep a lot, they don't sleep deeply: at the slightest sound they wake at once. So when a cat is sleeping, it's best not to disturb it.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白你知道吗？猫一天要睡十二到十六个小时，差不多是一天时间的三分之二。猫睡得这么多，是因为在外面生活的时候，猫要靠抓小动物生活，抓东西需要很多力气，所以它们要用睡觉来保存力气。另外，猫虽然睡得多，但睡得并不深，一有声音就会马上醒来。所以，猫睡觉的时候，最好不要打扰它。
Did you know? A cat sleeps twelve to sixteen hours a day — about two-thirds of its day. Cats sleep so much because, living outdoors, they depend on catching small animals, which takes a lot of energy, so they sleep to save their strength. Also, although cats sleep a lot, they don't sleep deeply: at the slightest sound they wake at once. So when a cat is sleeping, it's best not to disturb it.
第 19 段　Many people are polite to outsiders but seldom say "thank you" to their family. Mum cooks every day and we think that's only right; Dad fixes our bike and we think that's normal. In fact everything our family does for us deserves a "thank you". A simple word of thanks not only lets them feel their effort is noticed but also makes relationships at home better. Starting today, try saying "thank you" to your family more often.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白在家里，很多人对外人很客气，却很少对家人说“谢谢”。妈妈每天做饭，我们觉得是应该的；爸爸帮我们修好了自行车，我们也觉得很正常。其实，家人为我们做的每一件事，都值得说一声“谢谢”。一句简单的感谢，不但能让家人觉得自己的努力被看到了，也能让家里的关系变得更好。从今天开始，试着对家人多说一声“谢谢”吧。
Many people are polite to outsiders but seldom say "thank you" to their family. Mum cooks every day and we think that's only right; Dad fixes our bike and we think that's normal. In fact everything our family does for us deserves a "thank you". A simple word of thanks not only lets them feel their effort is noticed but also makes relationships at home better. Starting today, try saying "thank you" to your family more often.
