第 15 段　Recently many schools have banned pupils from bringing phones into class. Schools think playing with phones in lessons harms learning, and staring at phones after class cuts time for talking with classmates and exercising. Parents' views differ: some fully support it, thinking that with less phone time children will study harder; others worry they can't be reached in an emergency without a phone. To solve this, some schools have put a telephone in every classroom.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白最近，很多中小学规定学生不能带手机进教室。学校认为，学生上课时玩手机，会影响学习；下课以后一直看手机，也会减少和同学交流、运动的时间。对于这个规定，家长们的看法不太一样。有的家长很支持，认为孩子玩手机的时间少了，学习会更认真；也有的家长担心，孩子没有手机，有急事的时候联系不上。为了解决这个问题，有的学校在每个教室里放了一部电话。
Recently many schools have banned pupils from bringing phones into class. Schools think playing with phones in lessons harms learning, and staring at phones after class cuts time for talking with classmates and exercising. Parents' views differ: some fully support it, thinking that with less phone time children will study harder; others worry they can't be reached in an emergency without a phone. To solve this, some schools have put a telephone in every classroom.

第 17 段　Chopsticks are the tool Chinese people use most at meals, with a history of over three thousand years. Eating with them looks simple but actually needs the fingers to work together. Research has found that using chopsticks often makes the fingers more nimble and is good for children's brain development. In China there are also rules of politeness: for example, you mustn't point at people with chopsticks or stick them upright in your rice.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白筷子是中国人吃饭时最常用的工具，已经有三千多年的历史了。用筷子吃饭看起来简单，其实需要手指一起用力。有研究发现，经常用筷子，可以让手指更灵活，对孩子大脑的发展也有好处。另外，在中国，用筷子也有一些礼貌上的要求，比如不能用筷子指着别人，吃饭的时候也不能把筷子插在米饭里。
Chopsticks are the tool Chinese people use most at meals, with a history of over three thousand years. Eating with them looks simple but actually needs the fingers to work together. Research has found that using chopsticks often makes the fingers more nimble and is good for children's brain development. 第 19 段　Many people like comparing themselves with others: pay, homes, their children's marks. Comparing isn't bad in itself — seeing others' strengths can make us work harder. But if you always feel you fall short, your mood gets worse and worse. In fact everyone's situation is different: what others have, we don't necessarily need; what we have, others don't necessarily have. So the best comparison is between yourself today and yourself yesterday: as long as you improve a little each day, that's already very good.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白很多人喜欢拿自己和别人比较：比工资、比房子、比孩子的成绩。比较本来不是坏事，看到别人的优点，可以让我们更努力。但是，如果总是觉得自己不如别人，心情就会越来越差。其实，每个人的情况都不一样，别人有的，我们不一定需要；我们有的，别人也不一定有。所以，最好的比较，是拿今天的自己和昨天的自己比较，只要每天都有一点儿进步，就已经很好了。
Many people like comparing themselves with others: pay, homes, their children's marks. Comparing isn't bad in itself — seeing others' strengths can make us work harder. But if you always feel you fall short, your mood gets worse and worse. In fact everyone's situation is different: what others have, we don't necessarily need; what we have, others don't necessarily have. So the best comparison is between yourself today and yourself yesterday: as long as you improve a little each day, that's already very good.
