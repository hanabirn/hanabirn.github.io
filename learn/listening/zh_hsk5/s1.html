第 15 段　City people are busy every day: rushing out in the morning, grabbing something at noon, exhausted by the time they get home. So some have proposed the idea of "slow living". It doesn't mean doing nothing, or doing things slowly, but keeping some time in a busy life for what you truly enjoy — say, a weekend without your phone, a walk in the park, or an afternoon spent cooking a proper meal for your family. Many who have tried it find that when life slows down their mood actually improves, and so does their efficiency at work.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白现在城市里的人每天都很忙：早上急急忙忙地出门，中午随便吃点儿东西，晚上回到家已经很累了。于是，有人提出了“慢生活”的说法。慢生活并不是让人什么都不做，也不是让人做事很慢，而是希望人们在忙碌的生活中，给自己留一些时间，去做真正喜欢的事情。比如，周末不看手机，去公园走一走；或者花一个下午，认真地为家人做一顿饭。很多人试过以后发现，生活慢下来，心情反而变好了，工作的效率也提高了。
City people are busy every day: rushing out in the morning, grabbing something at noon, exhausted by the time they get home. So some have proposed the idea of "slow living". It doesn't mean doing nothing, or doing things slowly, but keeping some time in a busy life for what you truly enjoy — say, a weekend without your phone, a walk in the park, or an afternoon spent cooking a proper meal for your family. Many who have tried it find that when life slows down their mood actually improves, and so does their efficiency at work.

The neighbour thought this made good sense, and the next spring he planted a few trees in front of his own house.

第 18 段　People are more and more used to typing on computers and phones, and have fewer and fewer chances to write by hand. Experts think it's because typing only needs the pinyin, not every stroke of the character. To keep from forgetting, some people write a diary every day, and some have taken up calligraphy.
