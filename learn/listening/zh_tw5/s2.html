第 15 段　Welcome to today's programme. As it gets hotter, many people like iced drinks. But Chinese-medicine doctors warn that too much iced food upsets the stomach, especially after meals. They suggest warm water or room-temperature tea instead. In summer you sweat a lot, so drink plenty — but not too much at once: a small cup at a time, many times a day.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白各位觀眾，歡迎收聽今天的節目。最近天氣越來越熱，很多人喜歡喝冰的飲料。不過中醫師提醒，喝太多冰的東西會讓腸胃不舒服，特別是在吃飯以後。他建議大家，可以改喝溫水或是常溫的茶。另外，夏天流汗多，要記得多補充水分，但是一次不要喝太多，最好每次喝一小杯，一天喝很多次。
Welcome to today's programme. As it gets hotter, many people like iced drinks. But Chinese-medicine doctors warn that too much iced food upsets the stomach, especially after meals. They suggest warm water or room-temperature tea instead. In summer you sweat a lot, so drink plenty — but not too much at once: a small cup at a time, many times a day.
第 16 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男我在一家科技公司當工程師。以前我以為工程師只要會寫程式就好，工作以後才發現，跟同事溝通一樣重要。一個好的產品需要很多人合作，如果大家想法不一樣又不說清楚，最後常常要重做。所以現在每次開始一個新計畫，我都會先跟大家開會，把每個人的工作分清楚。
I'm an engineer at a tech company. I used to think engineers only needed to code; at work I found communicating with colleagues just as important. A good product needs many people working together; if people think differently and don't say so, things often have to be redone. So now I start every project with a meeting to divide everyone's work clearly.
第 17 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女台灣的便當文化很特別。很多上班族中午會買便當，一個便當有飯、一種肉和三四樣青菜，價錢大概一百塊左右。火車上賣的「台鐵便當」更是有名，有些人甚至專門坐火車就是為了吃便當。最近也有便當店推出比較健康的便當，少油少鹽，還會寫上熱量，吸引了很多注意身材的年輕人。
Taiwan's bento culture is special. Many office workers buy a bento for lunch: rice, a meat and three or four vegetables for around a hundred dollars. The Taiwan Railway bento sold on trains is famous — some people ride the train just to eat one. Recently shops have launched healthier bentos, less oily and salty, with calorie counts, drawing many figure-conscious young people.

男我從小就喜歡畫畫，大學讀的是設計。畢業以後，我在廣告公司工作了三年，每天都在幫客戶設計他們想要的東西。後來我覺得，自己真正想畫的東西越來越少了，所以我辭職開了一個網路頻道，教大家畫簡單的插畫。沒想到一年下來，已經有十萬人訂閱，我也開始出版自己的書。
I've loved drawing since childhood and studied design at university. After graduating I worked at an ad agency for three years, designing what clients wanted. Then I felt I was drawing less and less of what I really wanted, so I quit and started an online channel teaching simple illustration. To my surprise, a year later it had a hundred thousand subscribers, and I've begun publishing my own books.
