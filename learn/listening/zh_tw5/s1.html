第 15 段　In Taiwan, besides mooncakes, the Mid-Autumn Festival has a special custom: barbecues. On the festival evening many families grill outside their homes or in parks, eating while they look at the moon. Where did it come from? Some say an advert over thirty years ago got people barbecuing at Mid-Autumn; others think it's because the festival falls just as the weather turns cool, perfect for being outdoors. Whatever the reason, barbecuing has become how many Taiwanese celebrate. But people are reminded to be careful and to take their rubbish away afterwards.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白在台灣，中秋節除了吃月餅以外，還有一個特別的習慣，那就是烤肉。每到中秋節晚上，很多家庭會在家門口或者公園裡烤肉，一邊吃一邊看月亮。這個習慣是怎麼來的呢？有人說，是三十多年前一個廣告讓大家開始在中秋節烤肉。也有人認為，中秋節正好是天氣開始變涼的時候，很適合在外面活動。不管原因是什麼，烤肉已經成了很多台灣人過中秋的方式。不過，也有人提醒，烤肉的時候要注意安全，吃完以後也要把垃圾帶走。
In Taiwan, besides mooncakes, the Mid-Autumn Festival has a special custom: barbecues. On the festival evening many families grill outside their homes or in parks, eating while they look at the moon. Where did it come from? Some say an advert over thirty years ago got people barbecuing at Mid-Autumn; others think it's because the festival falls just as the weather turns cool, perfect for being outdoors. Whatever the reason, barbecuing has become how many Taiwanese celebrate. But people are reminded to be careful and to take their rubbish away afterwards.
第 16 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
男在台灣，有很多人的夢想是騎腳踏車環島。環島一次大約一千公里，一般需要九到十二天。路上可以看到高山、大海和各種不同的風景，還可以吃到各地有名的小吃。很多人說，環島最難的不是體力，而是堅持下去的決心。現在，有不少公司提供環島的服務，幫騎車的人安排住的地方、幫忙送行李，讓更多人有機會完成這個夢想。
In Taiwan many people dream of cycling round the island. The circuit is about a thousand kilometres and usually takes nine to twelve days, with mountains, sea and all kinds of scenery along the way, and famous local snacks to eat. Many say the hardest part isn't fitness but the determination to keep going. Now quite a few companies offer round-island services, arranging accommodation and carrying luggage, so more people can fulfil the dream.
第 17 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女在台北一條小路上，有一家只賣舊書的書店。老闆陳先生以前是一名工程師，因為太喜歡書，五十歲那年決定不做了，開了這家店。店裡的書都是別人不要的舊書，他一本一本地整理好，再用很便宜的價格賣出去。陳先生說，開書店賺不了什麼錢，但是每次看到有人在店裡找到自己喜歡的書，他就覺得很值得。
In a little lane in Taipei there is a shop that sells only second-hand books. The owner, Mr Chen, used to be an engineer; he loved books so much that at fifty he quit his job and opened the shop. Its books are ones other people no longer wanted; he tidies them one by one and sells them very cheaply. Mr Chen says a bookshop hardly makes money, but whenever someone finds a book they love there, it feels worth it.
第 18 段　Taiwan's high-mountain tea is famous abroad. It is usually grown high in the mountains, where mornings and evenings differ greatly in temperature and there is often cloud, so the leaves grow slowly and taste more fragrant. But growing it is hard work: farmers work on the mountains and the leaves can only be picked by hand. So high-mountain tea usually costs much more than ordinary tea.
逐字稿（聽完再看）
旁白台灣的高山茶在國外很有名。這種茶一般種在很高的山上。因為山上早上和晚上的溫度差別很大，又常常有雲，所以茶葉長得比較慢，味道也比較香。不過，種高山茶很辛苦，農民要在山上工作，摘茶葉也只能用手。所以，高山茶的價格通常比一般的茶高很多。
Taiwan's high-mountain tea is famous abroad. It is usually grown high in the mountains, where mornings and evenings differ greatly in temperature and there is often cloud, so the leaves grow slowly and taste more fragrant. But growing it is hard work: farmers work on the mountains and the leaves can only be picked by hand. So high-mountain tea usually costs much more than ordinary tea.
第 19 段　
逐字稿（聽完再看）
女台灣的中小學大多有營養午餐。學生每天中午在教室裡一起吃飯，飯菜由學校的廚房或外面的公司準備。營養午餐的菜單通常由營養師安排，每天都有飯、肉、青菜、湯和水果。吃完飯以後，學生要自己把碗洗乾淨，再一起打掃教室。很多老師認為，這不只是吃一頓飯，也是讓孩子學會照顧自己、和別人合作的好機會。
Most primary and secondary schools in Taiwan serve a nutritious school lunch. Pupils eat together in the classroom at noon, with food prepared by the school kitchen or an outside company. The menu is usually planned by a dietitian: rice, meat, vegetables, soup and fruit every day. After eating, pupils wash their own bowls and clean the classroom together. Many teachers think it's not just a meal but a good chance for children to learn to look after themselves and work with others.
