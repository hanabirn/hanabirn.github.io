一碗滷肉飯

A bowl of braised pork rice — Chinese reading, HSK 4

文章

如果要選一道最能代表臺灣的小吃，很多人會說是滷肉飯。一碗白飯上面放著滷得香香的豬肉，再加一顆滷蛋，簡單卻讓人吃得很滿足。
rú guǒ yào xuǎn yí dào zuì néng dài biǎo Tái wān de xiǎo chī hěn duō rén huì shuō shì lǔ ròu fàn yì wǎn bái fàn shàng miàn fàng zhe lǔ de xiāng xiāng de zhū ròu zài jiā yì kē lǔ dàn jiǎn dān què ràng rén chī de hěn mǎn zú
If you had to pick one snack that best represents Taiwan, many people would say braised pork rice. A bowl of white rice topped with fragrant braised pork, plus a braised egg — simple, yet very satisfying.
我外婆滷肉的時候，一定要用醬油、冰糖和紅蔥頭，小火慢慢煮兩三個小時。她說，好吃的祕密就是耐心，急不來。
wǒ wài pó lǔ ròu de shí hòu yí dìng yào yòng jiàng yóu bīng táng hé hóng cōng tóu xiǎo huǒ màn màn zhǔ liǎng sān gè xiǎo shí tā shuō hǎo chī de mì mì jiù shì nài xīn jí bù lái
When my grandmother braises pork, she always uses soy sauce, rock sugar and shallots and simmers it on a low flame for two or three hours. She says the secret of good flavour is patience — you can't rush it.
現在外面的滷肉飯一碗只要三、四十塊，但是對我來說，外婆做的味道是任何店都比不上的。
xiàn zài wài miàn de lǔ ròu fàn yì wǎn zhǐ yào sān sì shí kuài dàn shì duì wǒ lái shuō wài pó zuò de wèi dào shì rèn hé diàn dōu bǐ bú shàng de
Now a bowl outside costs only thirty or forty dollars, but for me no shop can match the taste of my grandmother's.
