環島

Round the island — Chinese reading, HSK 4

文章

很多臺灣年輕人都有一個夢想：騎腳踏車環島一圈。去年暑假，我和大學同學終於完成了這個計畫，十二天騎了將近一千公里。
hěn duō Tái wān nián qīng rén dōu yǒu yí gè mèng xiǎng qí jiǎo tà chē huán dǎo yì quān qù nián shǔ jià wǒ hé dà xué tóng xué zhōng yú wán chéng le zhè ge jì huà shí èr tiān qí le jiāng jìn yì qiān gōng lǐ
Many young Taiwanese share a dream: cycling round the whole island. Last summer my university friends and I finally did it, riding nearly a thousand kilometres in twelve days.
最辛苦的是東部的蘇花公路，一邊是山，一邊是海，有很多上坡，騎得腿都快斷了。不過，每天晚上住進民宿，吃到當地的美食，所有的累都不見了。
zuì xīn kǔ de shì dōng bù de sū huā gōng lù yì biān shì shān yì biān shì hǎi yǒu hěn duō shàng pō qí de tuǐ dōu kuài duàn le bú guò měi tiān wǎn shàng zhù jìn mín sù chī dào dāng dì de měi shí suǒ yǒu de lèi dōu bú jiàn le
The hardest part was the Suhua Highway in the east — mountains on one side, sea on the other, with endless climbs that nearly finished off our legs. But every evening, checking into a guesthouse and eating the local food, all the tiredness disappeared.
這趟旅行讓我更了解自己的家鄉，也讓我相信，只要不放棄，再遠的路也能走完。
zhè tàng lǚ xíng ràng wǒ gèng liǎo jiě zì jǐ de jiā xiāng yě ràng wǒ xiāng xìn zhǐ yào bú fàng qì zài yuǎn de lù yě néng zǒu wán
The trip helped me understand my homeland better, and convinced me that as long as you don't give up, you can finish any road, however long.
