跟室友合租

Sharing a flat — Chinese reading, HSK 4

文章

大學畢業以後，我在臺北找到工作，因為房租太貴，只好跟兩個朋友合租一間三房的公寓。一開始大家都很客氣，可是住久了，問題就慢慢出現了。
dà xué bì yè yǐ hòu wǒ zài Tái běi zhǎo dào gōng zuò yīn wèi fáng zū tài guì zhǐ hǎo gēn liǎng gè péng yǒu hé zū yì jiān sān fáng de gōng yù yì kāi shǐ dà jiā dōu hěn kè qì kě shì zhù jiǔ le wèn tí jiù màn màn chū xiàn le
After graduating I found a job in Taipei; because rent was so expensive, I had to share a three-bedroom flat with two friends. At first everyone was polite, but as time went on problems slowly appeared.
有人洗完碗不擦乾，有人半夜還在客廳打電動。後來我們開了一次會，決定把打掃的工作輪流分配，晚上十一點以後說話也要小聲一點。
yǒu rén xǐ wán wǎn bù cā gàn yǒu rén bàn yè hái zài kè tīng dǎ diàn dòng hòu lái wǒ men kāi le yí cì huì jué dìng bǎ dǎ sǎo de gōng zuò lún liú fēn pèi wǎn shàng shí yī diǎn yǐ hòu shuō huà yě yào xiǎo shēng yì diǎn
Someone would wash up without drying, someone else would play video games in the living room in the middle of the night. Later we held a meeting and decided to take turns with the cleaning, and to keep our voices down after eleven.
現在大家相處得很愉快，週末還常常一起煮火鍋。我覺得，住在一起最重要的是互相體諒。
xiàn zài dà jiā xiāng chǔ de hěn yú kuài zhōu mò hái cháng cháng yì qǐ zhǔ huǒ guō wǒ jué de zhù zài yì qǐ zuì zhòng yào de shì hù xiāng tǐ liàng
Now we get on very well and often make hotpot together at weekends. I think the most important thing about living together is being considerate of each other.

生詞

合租 hézū　to share rent
客氣 kèqì　polite
出現 chūxiàn　to appear
打電動 dǎdiàndòng　to play video games
輪流 lúnliú　to take turns
分配 fēnpèi　to allocate
相處 xiāngchǔ　to get along
體諒 tǐliàng　to be understanding
