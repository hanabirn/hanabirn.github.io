參加社團

Joining a club — Chinese reading, HSK 4

文章

上大學以前，我只知道讀書和考試。大一的時候，學長推薦我參加學校的登山社，我抱著試試看的想法加入了。
shàng dà xué yǐ qián wǒ zhǐ zhī dào dú shū hé kǎo shì dà yī de shí hòu xué zhǎng tuī jiàn wǒ cān jiā xué xiào de dēng shān shè wǒ bào zhe shì shi kàn de xiǎng fǎ jiā rù le
Before university, all I knew was studying and exams. In my first year, an older student recommended the school's mountaineering club, and I joined thinking I'd give it a try.
第一次爬的是陽明山的七星山，我爬到一半就喘得說不出話來。社員們沒有笑我，反而一直鼓勵我，還幫我拿背包。到了山頂，看到整個臺北盆地，我覺得一切辛苦都值得了。
dì yī cì pá de shì Yáng míng shān de qī xīng shān wǒ pá dào yí bàn jiù chuǎn de shuō bù chū huà lái shè yuán men méi yǒu xiào wǒ fǎn ér yì zhí gǔ lì wǒ hái bāng wǒ ná bēi bāo dào le shān dǐng kàn dào zhěng gè Tái běi pén dì wǒ jué de yí qiè xīn kǔ dōu zhí de le
The first climb was Qixing Mountain in Yangmingshan; halfway up I was so out of breath I couldn't speak. The members didn't laugh at me — instead they kept encouraging me and even carried my backpack. At the top, looking out over the whole Taipei Basin, I felt every bit of effort had been worth it.
四年下來，我爬了三十多座山，也交到了一群好朋友。
sì nián xià lái wǒ pá le sān shí duō zuò shān yě jiāo dào le yì qún hǎo péng yǒu
Over four years I climbed more than thirty mountains and made a group of good friends.
