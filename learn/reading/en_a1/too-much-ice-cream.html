Too Much Ice Cream

冰淇淋吃太多了

— English reading, A1

文章

Yesterday was very hot. After lunch, I ate three ice creams with my friends.
昨天很熱。午餐後，我和朋友們吃了三支冰淇淋。
During math class, my stomach started to hurt. I told my teacher, and she sent me to the school nurse.
上數學課的時候，我的肚子開始痛。我告訴老師，她叫我去找學校護士。
The nurse asked me, “What did you eat today?” When I told her, she smiled and said, “That's too much ice cream!”
護士問我：「你今天吃了什麼？」我告訴她以後，她笑著說：「冰淇淋吃太多了！」
She gave me a glass of warm water and let me rest for an hour. Next time, I'll only eat one ice cream.
她給我一杯溫水，讓我休息了一個小時。下次我只吃一支冰淇淋就好。

生詞

during　在……期間
stomach　肚子；胃
hurt　痛
send　送；叫（某人）去
nurse　護士
smile　微笑
warm　溫暖的；溫的
rest　休息
