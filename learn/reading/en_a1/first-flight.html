My First Flight

我第一次坐飛機

— English reading, A1

文章

Last winter, I flew to Seoul to visit my uncle. It was my first time on a plane.
去年冬天，我搭飛機去首爾看叔叔。那是我第一次坐飛機。
At the airport, I was a little nervous. But the flight attendant was very kind and showed me my seat. I had a window seat!
在機場的時候我有點緊張。但是空服員很親切，帶我找到座位。我坐靠窗的位子！
When the plane took off, my ears hurt a little. Then I looked out of the window. We were above the clouds, and they looked like cotton.
飛機起飛的時候，我的耳朵有點痛。然後我看向窗外。我們在雲的上面，雲看起來像棉花一樣。
The flight took three hours. My uncle was waiting for me at the airport with a big sign.
飛行花了三個小時。叔叔拿著一個大牌子在機場等我。

生詞

fly　搭飛機去；飛
uncle　叔叔；伯伯；舅舅
airport　機場
nervous　緊張的
seat　座位
take off　起飛
above　在……上面
sign　牌子
