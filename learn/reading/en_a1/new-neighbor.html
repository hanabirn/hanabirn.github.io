Our New Neighbor

我們的新鄰居

— English reading, A1

文章

Last month, a new neighbor moved into the house next to ours. Her name is Mrs. Green, and she is eighty years old.
上個月，有一位新鄰居搬進了我們隔壁的房子。她叫格林太太，今年八十歲。
Mrs. Green lives alone with her cat. She has a beautiful garden with lots of flowers. Every morning, she waters them.
格林太太一個人和她的貓住。她有一座開滿花的漂亮花園。她每天早上都會澆花。
On Saturday, I saw her with three heavy bags from the store. I helped her carry them into her kitchen.
星期六，我看到她提著三個從商店買回來的重袋子。我幫她把袋子提進廚房。
The next day, she brought us some cookies. They were delicious! Now we often talk over the fence.
隔天，她送了一些餅乾給我們。好好吃！現在我們常常隔著圍籬聊天。

生詞

neighbor　鄰居
move　搬（家）
next to　在……旁邊
alone　獨自；一個人
garden　花園
water　澆水
heavy　重的
carry　提；搬
