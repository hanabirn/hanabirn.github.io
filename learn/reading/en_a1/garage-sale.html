The Garage Sale

車庫拍賣

— English reading, A1

文章

On Saturday, our neighbors had a garage sale. They put old things on tables in front of their house.
星期六，我們的鄰居辦了一場車庫拍賣（在自家門口賣舊東西）。他們把舊東西放在家門前的桌子上。
There were books, toys, clothes and a lamp. Everything was very cheap.
有書、玩具、衣服和一盞檯燈。每樣東西都很便宜。
I saw an old guitar. The price was twenty dollars, but I only had fifteen. The man said, “OK, fifteen is fine.”
我看到一把舊吉他。價錢是二十元，可是我只有十五元。那位先生說：「好啊，十五元也可以。」
I also bought two books for one dollar each. Now I'm learning to play the guitar every day.
我還買了兩本書，一本一元。現在我每天都在學彈吉他。
