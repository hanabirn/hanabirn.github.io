Camping by the Lake

湖邊露營

— English reading, A1

文章

Last summer, my family went camping by a lake in the mountains. We drove there for three hours.
去年夏天，我們全家去山裡的一座湖邊露營。我們開了三個小時的車。
First, my dad and I put up the tent. It was hard, but we did it in thirty minutes. Then we went swimming in the lake. The water was very cold!
首先，我和爸爸把帳篷搭起來。很難，但是我們三十分鐘就搭好了。然後我們去湖裡游泳。湖水好冰！
At night, we made a fire and cooked hot dogs. There were thousands of stars in the sky.
晚上我們生了火，煮熱狗。天上有成千上萬顆星星。
The only problem was the mosquitoes. In the morning, my legs were covered in bites.
唯一的問題是蚊子。早上起來，我的腿上全是蚊子咬的包。
