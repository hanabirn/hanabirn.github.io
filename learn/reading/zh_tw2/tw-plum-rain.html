梅雨季

The plum-rain season — Chinese reading, HSK 2

文章

每年五月到六月是臺灣的梅雨季。這個時候常常下雨，有時候一下就是一個星期。
měi nián wǔ yuè dào liù yuè shì Tái wān de méi yǔ jì zhè ge shí hòu cháng cháng xià yǔ yǒu shí hòu yí xià jiù shì yí gè xīng qí
Every year May to June is Taiwan's plum-rain season. It rains a lot then — sometimes for a whole week at a time.
衣服洗了以後很難乾，房間裡也很溼。媽媽每天都要開除溼機，不然牆上會長東西。我不喜歡梅雨季，因為不能出去打球。
yī fú xǐ le yǐ hòu hěn nán gàn fáng jiān lǐ yě hěn shī mā ma měi tiān dōu yào kāi chú shī jī bù rán qiáng shàng huì zhǎng dōng xi wǒ bù xǐ huān méi yǔ jì yīn wèi bù néng chū qù dǎ qiú
Washed clothes are slow to dry and the rooms are damp. Mom has to run the dehumidifier every day, or things grow on the walls. I don't like the plum-rain season because I can't go out to play ball.
