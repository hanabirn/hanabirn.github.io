逛夜市

Going round the night market — Chinese reading, HSK 2

文章

週末晚上，我常常和同學去逛夜市。夜市裡的東西又多又便宜，人也很多。
zhōu mò wǎn shàng wǒ cháng cháng hé tóng xué qù guàng yè shì yè shì lǐ de dōng xi yòu duō yòu pián yí rén yě hěn duō
On weekend evenings I often go round the night market with classmates. There's lots there, it's cheap, and it's crowded.
我最喜歡吃雞排和蚵仔煎，同學喜歡喝木瓜牛奶。吃完以後，我們還會玩一些小遊戲。上次我玩了三次，拿到一個很大的熊。
wǒ zuì xǐ huān chī jī pái hé é zǎi jiān tóng xué xǐ huān hē mù guā niú nǎi chī wán yǐ hòu wǒ men hái huì wán yì xiē xiǎo yóu xì shàng cì wǒ wán le sān cì ná dào yí gè hěn dà de xióng
My favourites are fried chicken cutlet and oyster omelette; my classmate likes papaya milk. After eating we play some little games too. Last time I played three times and won a huge teddy bear.
