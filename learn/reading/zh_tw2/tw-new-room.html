新房間

A new room — Chinese reading, HSK 2

文章

上個月我開始在臺中上大學，在學校附近租了一個房間。房間不大，但是有冷氣，也有一個小陽臺。
shàng gè yuè wǒ kāi shǐ zài Tái zhōng shàng dà xué zài xué xiào fù jìn zū le yí gè fáng jiān fáng jiān bú dà dàn shì yǒu lěng qì yě yǒu yí gè xiǎo yáng tái
Last month I started university in Taichung and rented a room near campus. It isn't big, but it has air conditioning and a small balcony.
房東是一位六十多歲的阿姨，她每個星期都送水果給我。晚上我常在陽臺上看書，可以看到很遠的山。我覺得住在這裡很快樂。
fáng dōng shì yí wèi liù shí duō suì de ā yí tā měi gè xīng qí dōu sòng shuǐ guǒ gěi wǒ wǎn shàng wǒ cháng zài yáng tái shàng kàn shū kě yǐ kàn dào hěn yuǎn de shān wǒ jué de zhù zài zhè lǐ hěn kuài lè
The landlady is in her sixties and brings me fruit every week. In the evening I often read on the balcony, and I can see the mountains far away. I'm very happy living here.
