學游泳

Learning to swim — Chinese reading, HSK 2

文章

我小時候很怕水，所以一直沒有學游泳。去年夏天，我終於報名了游泳課，每個星期上兩次。
wǒ xiǎo shí hòu hěn pà shuǐ suǒ yǐ yì zhí méi yǒu xué yóu yǒng qù nián xià tiān wǒ zhōng yú bào míng le yóu yǒng kè měi gè xīng qí shàng liǎng cì
I was afraid of water as a child, so I never learned to swim. Last summer I finally signed up for swimming lessons, twice a week.
教練非常有耐心，第一堂課只讓我們在水裡走路。一個月以後，我已經可以游二十五公尺了。現在游泳是我最喜歡的運動，身體也比以前好。
jiào liàn fēi cháng yǒu nài xīn dì yī táng kè zhǐ ràng wǒ men zài shuǐ lǐ zǒu lù yí gè yuè yǐ hòu wǒ yǐ jīng kě yǐ yóu èr shí wǔ gōng chǐ le xiàn zài yóu yǒng shì wǒ zuì xǐ huān de yùn dòng shēn tǐ yě bǐ yǐ qián hǎo
The coach is very patient; in the first lesson he only had us walk in the water. A month later I could already swim twenty-five metres. Now swimming is my favourite sport, and I'm healthier than before.
