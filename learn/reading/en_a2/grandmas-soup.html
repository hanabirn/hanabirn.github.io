Grandma's Soup

奶奶的湯

— English reading, A2

文章

When I was a child, my grandmother made chicken soup every Sunday. The whole family sat at her big table and ate together.
我小時候，奶奶每個星期天都會煮雞湯。全家人圍坐在她的大桌子旁一起吃。
Last winter, I caught a bad cold. I called my grandmother and asked for her recipe. She laughed and said, "There is no recipe. I just cook."
去年冬天，我得了重感冒。我打電話給奶奶，向她要食譜。她笑著說：「沒有食譜，我就是這樣煮。」
So I visited her, and she taught me in her kitchen. We cut carrots, onions and potatoes, and we cooked the soup slowly for two hours.
所以我去找她，她在廚房裡教我。我們切了紅蘿蔔、洋蔥和馬鈴薯，用小火把湯慢慢煮了兩個小時。
My soup is not as good as hers yet. But I have written down every step, and now I make it whenever someone in my family feels sick.
我的湯還沒有她煮的好喝。不過我把每個步驟都寫了下來，現在只要家裡有人不舒服，我就會煮。
