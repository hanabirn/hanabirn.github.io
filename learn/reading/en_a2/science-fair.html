The Science Fair

科學展覽

— English reading, A2

文章

Every year, our school has a science fair in March. This year, my friend Maya and I decided to work together.
我們學校每年三月都會舉辦科學展覽。今年，我和朋友瑪雅決定一起合作。
We wanted to find out which drink is the worst for your teeth. We put eggshells into cola, orange juice, milk and water for a week, because eggshells are similar to teeth.
我們想找出哪一種飲料對牙齒最不好。因為蛋殼和牙齒很像，我們把蛋殼分別放進可樂、柳橙汁、牛奶和水裡，泡了一個星期。
The results surprised us. The eggshell in cola turned brown, but the one in orange juice became the softest. The shells in milk and water didn't change at all.
結果讓我們很意外。泡可樂的蛋殼變成了褐色，但泡柳橙汁的那個變得最軟。泡在牛奶和水裡的蛋殼完全沒有變化。
We didn't win first prize, but the judges liked our clear poster. Since then, I have drunk much less juice.
我們沒有得到第一名，不過評審很喜歡我們清楚的海報。從那之後，我果汁喝得少多了。
