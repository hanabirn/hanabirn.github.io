坐地铁

Taking the subway — Chinese reading, HSK 2

文章

我每天坐地铁去上班。从我家到公司要换一次车，一共四十分钟。
wǒ měi tiān zuò dì tiě qù shàng bān cóng wǒ jiā dào gōng sī yào huàn yí cì chē yí gòng sì shí fēn zhōng
I take the subway to work every day. From my home to the office I change once; it takes forty minutes in all.
早上八点左右地铁里的人最多，常常没有座位。所以我七点就出门，这时候人比较少。
zǎo shang bā diǎn zuǒ yòu dì tiě lǐ de rén zuì duō cháng cháng méi yǒu zuò wèi suǒ yǐ wǒ qī diǎn jiù chū mén zhè shí hou rén bǐ jiào shǎo
Around eight in the morning the subway is most crowded, and there are often no seats. So I leave at seven, when there are fewer people.
在地铁上我喜欢听音乐或者看新闻。我觉得坐地铁比开车好，不用担心堵车。
zài dì tiě shàng wǒ xǐ huan tīng yīn yuè huò zhě kàn xīn wén wǒ jué de zuò dì tiě bǐ kāi chē hǎo bú yòng dān xīn dǔ chē
On the subway I like listening to music or reading the news. I think the subway is better than driving: no worrying about traffic jams.

生詞

地铁 dìtiě　subway
换车 huànchē　to change trains
一共 yígòng　in total
左右 zuǒyòu　around, about
座位 zuòwèi　seat
出门 chūmén　to go out
新闻 xīnwén　news
堵车 dǔchē　traffic jam
