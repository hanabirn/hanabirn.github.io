一个人旅行

Travelling alone — Chinese reading, HSK 4

文章

以前我出去旅行，都是跟着旅行团，什么都不用自己安排。去年我第一次一个人去了成都，从订机票、找酒店到计划每天的路线，全部都是自己完成的。
yǐ qián wǒ chū qù lǚ xíng dōu shì gēn zhe lǚ xíng tuán shén me dōu bú yòng zì jǐ ān pái qù nián wǒ dì yī cì yí gè rén qù le Chéng dū cóng dìng jī piào zhǎo jiǔ diàn dào jì huà měi tiān de lù xiàn quán bù dōu shì zì jǐ wán chéng de
I used to travel with tour groups, never having to organise anything myself. Last year I went to Chengdu alone for the first time, and did everything myself — booking flights, finding a hotel, planning each day's route.
一个人旅行最大的好处是自由。看到喜欢的地方，我可以多待一会儿；累了就找个茶馆坐下来，跟当地的老人聊聊天。
yí gè rén lǚ xíng zuì dà de hǎo chu shì zì yóu kàn dào xǐ huan de dì fang wǒ kě yǐ duō dāi yí huì r lèi le jiù zhǎo gè chá guǎn zuò xià lai gēn dāng dì de lǎo rén liáo liao tiān
The biggest advantage of travelling alone is freedom. When I find a place I like, I can stay longer; when I'm tired I sit down in a teahouse and chat with the local old people.
当然，也有不方便的地方，比如吃饭的时候很难点很多菜，拍照也只能请别人帮忙。但是总的来说，这次经历让我变得更有信心了。
dāng rán yě yǒu bù fāng biàn de dì fang bǐ rú chī fàn de shí hou hěn nán diǎn hěn duō cài pāi zhào yě zhǐ néng qǐng bié ren bāng máng dàn shì zǒng de lái shuō zhè cì jīng lì ràng wǒ biàn de gèng yǒu xìn xīn le
Of course there are drawbacks: at meals it's hard to order lots of dishes, and for photos you have to ask strangers for help. Overall, though, the experience made me more confident.
