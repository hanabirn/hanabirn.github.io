二手市场

The second-hand market — Chinese reading, HSK 4

文章

每年六月，我们学校都会举办一次二手市场。快毕业的学生把用不着的书、自行车和小家电拿出来卖，价格一般只有新的三分之一。
měi nián liù yuè wǒ men xué xiào dōu huì jǔ bàn yí cì èr shǒu shì chǎng kuài bì yè de xué sheng bǎ yòng bu zháo de shū zì xíng chē hé xiǎo jiā diàn ná chū lái mài jià gé yì bān zhǐ yǒu xīn de sān fēn zhī yī
Every June our university holds a second-hand market. Students about to graduate bring out books, bikes and small appliances they no longer need, usually at a third of the new price.
今年我花了八十块钱买了一个台灯，又花两百块买了一辆自行车。卖自行车的是一位研究生，他告诉我车子虽然旧了，但是每个月都检查，骑起来很轻松。
jīn nián wǒ huā le bā shí kuài qián mǎi le yí gè tái dēng yòu huā liǎng bǎi kuài mǎi le yí liàng zì xíng chē mài zì xíng chē de shì yí wèi yán jiū shēng tā gào su wǒ chē zi suī rán jiù le dàn shì měi gè yuè dōu jiǎn chá qí qi lai hěn qīng sōng
This year I paid eighty yuan for a desk lamp and two hundred for a bike. The seller of the bike was a postgraduate, who told me that although it was old, he checked it every month and it rode easily.
我觉得二手市场不但能省钱，而且能减少浪费。东西还能用，就应该让它找到新的主人。
wǒ jué de èr shǒu shì chǎng bú dàn néng shěng qián ér qiě néng jiǎn shǎo làng fèi dōng xi hái néng yòng jiù yīng gāi ràng tā zhǎo dào xīn de zhǔ rén
I think a second-hand market not only saves money but also cuts down on waste. If something still works, it should find a new owner.
