租房子

Renting a flat — Chinese reading, HSK 4

文章

大学毕业以后，我来到北京工作，第一件麻烦事就是找房子。公司附近的房租太贵了，我只好在离公司一个小时远的地方租了一个小房间。
dà xué bì yè yǐ hòu wǒ lái dào Běi jīng gōng zuò dì yī jiàn má fan shì jiù shì zhǎo fáng zi gōng sī fù jìn de fáng zū tài guì le wǒ zhǐ hǎo zài lí gōng sī yí gè xiǎo shí yuǎn de dì fang zū le yí gè xiǎo fáng jiān
After graduating I came to Beijing to work, and the first headache was finding somewhere to live. Rents near the office were too high, so I had to rent a small room an hour away.
房间虽然不大，但是很干净，房东阿姨也很热情。她知道我一个人在外地，常常给我送饺子，有时候还提醒我天冷了要多穿衣服。
fáng jiān suī rán bú dà dàn shì hěn gān jìng fáng dōng ā yí yě hěn rè qíng tā zhī dào wǒ yí gè rén zài wài dì cháng cháng gěi wǒ sòng jiǎo zi yǒu shí hou hái tí xǐng wǒ tiān lěng le yào duō chuān yī fu
The room isn't big, but it's clean, and the landlady is very warm. Knowing I'm on my own far from home, she often brings me dumplings and sometimes reminds me to wrap up when it gets cold.
每天在地铁上花两个小时确实很累，可是我已经习惯了，还利用这段时间读完了好几本书。对我来说，这儿已经像半个家了。
měi tiān zài dì tiě shàng huā liǎng gè xiǎo shí què shí hěn lèi kě shì wǒ yǐ jīng xí guàn le hái lì yòng zhè duàn shí jiān dú wán le hǎo jǐ běn shū duì wǒ lái shuō zhè r yǐ jīng xiàng bàn gè jiā le
Spending two hours a day on the metro is certainly tiring, but I've got used to it, and I've used the time to finish quite a few books. For me this place already feels like half a home.
