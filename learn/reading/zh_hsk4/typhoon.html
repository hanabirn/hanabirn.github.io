台风来了

The typhoon is coming — Chinese reading, HSK 4

文章

上个星期，一个很强的台风经过我们城市。气象台提前三天就发出了提醒，学校停课，很多公司也让员工在家上班。
shàng gè xīng qī yí gè hěn qiáng de tái fēng jīng guò wǒ men chéng shì qì xiàng tái tí qián sān tiān jiù fā chū le tí xǐng xué xiào tíng kè hěn duō gōng sī yě ràng yuán gōng zài jiā shàng bān
Last week a powerful typhoon passed through our city. The weather bureau issued a warning three days ahead; schools closed and many companies had staff work from home.
台风来之前，超市里的面包、方便面和矿泉水几乎被抢光了。我和妻子也买了不少吃的，还把阳台上的花盆都搬进了屋里。
tái fēng lái zhī qián chāo shì lǐ de miàn bāo fāng biàn miàn hé kuàng quán shuǐ jī hū bèi qiǎng guāng le wǒ hé qī zi yě mǎi le bù shǎo chī de hái bǎ yáng tái shàng de huā pén dōu bān jìn le wū lǐ
Before the typhoon arrived, the bread, instant noodles and bottled water in the supermarkets were almost cleared out. My wife and I also bought plenty of food and moved all the flowerpots from the balcony indoors.
那天晚上风特别大，窗户一直响，我们几乎没睡着。第二天早上出门一看，路边倒了好几棵树。还好大家都准备得很充分，城市里没有人受伤。
nà tiān wǎn shang fēng tè bié dà chuāng hu yì zhí xiǎng wǒ men jī hū méi shuì zháo dì èr tiān zǎo shang chū mén yí kàn lù biān dào le hǎo jǐ kē shù hái hǎo dà jiā dōu zhǔn bèi de hěn chōng fèn chéng shì lǐ méi yǒu rén shòu shāng
That night the wind was fierce and the windows rattled constantly; we hardly slept. Going out the next morning, we saw several trees down by the road. Fortunately everyone was well prepared, and nobody in the city was hurt.

生詞

台风 táifēng　typhoon
经过 jīngguò　to pass through
气象台 qìxiàngtái　weather bureau
员工 yuángōng　employee
几乎 jīhū　almost
抢 qiǎng　to snatch, rush to buy
还好 háihǎo　fortunately
受伤 shòushāng　to be injured
