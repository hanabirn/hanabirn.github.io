Our New Wellness Program

我們的新健康計畫

— English reading, TOEIC

文章

This spring, Hartley Insurance is launching a wellness program to help employees stay healthy. Participation is voluntary, but we hope many of you will join.
今年春天，哈特利保險推出一項健康計畫，幫助員工保持健康。參加與否自由，但我們希望很多人加入。
The program includes weekly yoga classes, free health checks and advice from a nutritionist. Yoga classes are held in the training room every Tuesday at lunchtime.
計畫內容包括每週瑜伽課、免費健康檢查和營養師的建議。瑜伽課每週二午餐時間在訓練室上課。
Employees who attend at least ten classes will receive a gift card worth fifty dollars.
參加至少十堂課的員工將獲得價值五十美元的禮物卡。
Studies show that healthy workers take fewer sick days and feel more satisfied with their jobs. We believe the program will benefit everyone.
研究顯示，健康的員工請病假較少，對工作也更滿意。我們相信這項計畫對每個人都有益。
