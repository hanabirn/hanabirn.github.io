Notice to Residents: Lobby Renovation

住戶通知：大廳整修

— English reading, TOEIC

文章

Starting Monday, May 6, the lobby of Parkview Apartments will be closed for renovation. The work is expected to take about three weeks.
自五月六日星期一起，公園景公寓的大廳將因整修而關閉。工程預計約需三週。
During this time, residents should use the side entrance on Elm Street. Mail will be delivered to temporary mailboxes next to the elevators.
這段期間，住戶請使用榆樹街的側門。郵件將投遞到電梯旁的臨時信箱。
Work will take place on weekdays between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m., so some noise is unavoidable. No work will be done on weekends.
工程於平日上午九點至下午五點進行，因此難免會有一些噪音。週末不施工。
We apologize for any inconvenience. If you have questions, please contact the management office at extension 210.
造成不便，敬請見諒。如有疑問，請撥分機 210 聯絡管理室。
