明天考试

A test tomorrow — Chinese reading, TW HSK 1–2

文章

明天有中文考试，我有一点紧张。下课以后，我和同学小明一起去图书馆。
Míng tiān yǒu zhōng wén kǎo shì wǒ yǒu yì diǎn jǐn zhāng Xià kè yǐ hòu wǒ hé tóng xué Xiǎo míng yì qǐ qù tú shū guǎn
There's a Chinese test tomorrow, and I'm a bit nervous. After class my classmate Xiaoming and I went to the library.
我们在图书馆准备了三个小时。小明很会写汉字，他教我写难的字。我说得比较好，所以我帮他练习说话。
Wǒ men zài tú shū guǎn zhǔn bèi le sān ge xiǎo shí Xiǎo míng hěn huì xiě hàn zì tā jiāo wǒ xiě nán de zì Wǒ shuō de bǐ jiào hǎo suǒ yǐ wǒ bāng tā liàn xí shuō huà
We studied in the library for three hours. Xiaoming is good at writing characters, so he taught me the hard ones. I speak better, so I helped him practise speaking.
晚上九点我们才离开图书馆。我觉得明天的考试没问题了！
Wǎn shàng jiǔ diǎn wǒ men cái lí kāi tú shū guǎn Wǒ jué de míng tiān de kǎo shì méi wèn tí le
We didn't leave the library until nine in the evening. I think tomorrow's test will be fine!

生詞

紧张 jǐnzhāng　nervous
同学 tóngxué　classmate
图书馆 túshūguǎn　library
准备 zhǔnbèi　to prepare
教 jiāo　to teach
练习 liànxí　to practise
离开 líkāi　to leave
