爸爸开始跑步

Dad starts running — Chinese reading, TW HSK 1–2

文章

我爸爸今年五十岁。以前他很喜欢吃肉，也不常运动。
Wǒ bà ba jīn nián wǔ shí suì Yǐ qián tā hěn xǐ huān chī ròu yě bù cháng yùn dòng
My dad is fifty this year. He used to love eating meat and didn't exercise much.
上个月医生说：「你太胖了，要多运动。」
Shàng ge yuè yī shēng shuō Nǐ tài pàng le yào duō yùn dòng
Last month the doctor said: “You're too heavy. You need to exercise more.”
所以爸爸每天早上六点起床，去公园跑步。第一天他只跑了十分钟就累了。现在他可以跑半个小时。
Suǒ yǐ bà ba měi tiān zǎo shàng liù diǎn qǐ chuáng qù gōng yuán pǎo bù Dì yī tiān tā zhǐ pǎo le shí fēn zhōng jiù lèi le Xiàn zài tā kě yǐ pǎo bàn ge xiǎo shí
So Dad gets up at six every morning and goes running in the park. On the first day he was tired after only ten minutes. Now he can run for half an hour.
他也开始多吃青菜和水果。妈妈说爸爸的身体比以前好多了。
Tā yě kāi shǐ duō chī qīng cài hé shuǐ guǒ Mā ma shuō bà ba de shēn tǐ bǐ yǐ qián hǎo duō le
He has also started eating more vegetables and fruit. Mom says Dad is much healthier than before.

生詞

岁 suì　years old
肉 ròu　meat
运动 yùndòng　to exercise; sport
医生 yīshēng　doctor
胖 pàng　fat, overweight
跑步 pǎobù　to run, to jog
累 lèi　tired
青菜 qīngcài　(green) vegetables
