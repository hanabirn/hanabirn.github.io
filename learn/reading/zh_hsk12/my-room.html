我的房间

My room — Chinese reading, TW HSK 1–2

文章

我的房间不大，可是很舒服。房间里有一张床、一张桌子和两把椅子。
Wǒ de fáng jiān bú dà kě shì hěn shū fú Fáng jiān lǐ yǒu yì zhāng chuáng yì zhāng zhuō zi hé liǎng bǎ yǐ zi
My room isn't big, but it's very comfortable. In the room there's a bed, a desk and two chairs.
桌子在窗户旁边。我每天晚上在这里写功课。窗户外面有一棵大树。
Zhuō zi zài chuāng hù páng biān Wǒ měi tiān wǎn shàng zài zhè lǐ xiě gōng kè Chuāng hù wài miàn yǒu yì kē dà shù
The desk is next to the window. I do my homework here every evening. Outside the window there's a big tree.
床上有一只白色的小狗。牠不是真的狗，是我八岁的时候妈妈送我的。
Chuáng shàng yǒu yì zhī bái sè de xiǎo gǒu Tā bú shì zhēn de gǒu shì wǒ bā suì de shí hòu mā ma sòng wǒ de
On the bed there's a little white dog. It isn't a real dog — my mom gave it to me when I was eight.
周末我喜欢在房间里看书、听音乐。这是我最喜欢的地方。
Zhōu mò wǒ xǐ huān zài fáng jiān lǐ kàn shū tīng yīn lè Zhè shì wǒ zuì xǐ huān de dì fāng
At the weekend I like reading and listening to music in my room. It's my favorite place.

生詞

房间 fángjiān　room
舒服 shūfú　comfortable
张 zhāng　measure word for flat things (beds, tables, cards)
椅子 yǐzi　chair
窗户 chuānghù　window
功课 gōngkè　homework
送 sòng　to give (as a present)
地方 dìfāng　place
