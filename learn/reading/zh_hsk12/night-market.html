去夜市

At the night market — Chinese reading, TW HSK 1–2

文章

星期五晚上，我和两个朋友去夜市。夜市里人很多，也很热闹。
Xīng qí wǔ wǎn shàng wǒ hé liǎng ge péng yǒu qù yè shì Yè shì lǐ rén hěn duō yě hěn rè nào
On Friday evening I went to the night market with two friends. There were lots of people and it was very lively.
我们先吃了鸡排，然后买了一杯珍珠奶茶。鸡排很大，一块只要八十块。
Wǒ men xiān chī le jī pái rán hòu mǎi le yì bēi zhēn zhū nǎi chá Jī pái hěn dà yí kuài zhǐ yào bā shí kuài
First we ate fried chicken, then we bought a bubble tea. The chicken was big — only eighty dollars a piece.
小美想吃臭豆腐，可是我不喜欢它的味道。所以我去买了烤玉米。
Xiǎo měi xiǎng chī chòu dòu fu kě shì wǒ bù xǐ huān tā de wèi dào Suǒ yǐ wǒ qù mǎi le kǎo yù mǐ
Xiaomei wanted stinky tofu, but I don't like how it smells. So I went and bought grilled corn.
吃完东西，我们还玩了游戏。十点我们才回家，大家都很开心。
Chī wán dōng xi wǒ men hái wán le yóu xì Shí diǎn wǒ men cái huí jiā dà jiā dōu hěn kāi xīn
After eating we played some games too. We didn't go home until ten, and everyone was very happy.

生詞

热闹 rènào　lively, bustling
先 xiān　first
然后 ránhòu　then, after that
杯 bēi　cup (measure word)
味道 wèidào　taste, smell
东西 dōngxi　thing(s); food
开心 kāixīn　happy
