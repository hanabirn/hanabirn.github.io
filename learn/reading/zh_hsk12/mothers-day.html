母亲节的礼物

A Mother's Day present — Chinese reading, TW HSK 1–2

文章

下个星期天是母亲节。我和妹妹想给妈妈买礼物。
Xià ge xīng qí tiān shì mǔ qīn jié Wǒ hé mèi mei xiǎng gěi mā ma mǎi lǐ wù
Next Sunday is Mother's Day. My little sister and I want to buy Mom a present.
我们先去百货公司看衣服，可是衣服太贵了。一件要两千块。
Wǒ men xiān qù bǎi huò gōng sī kàn yī fú kě shì yī fú tài guì le Yí jiàn yào liǎng qiān kuài
First we went to the department store to look at clothes, but they were too expensive. One piece cost two thousand dollars.
后来我们去了花店。店里的花很漂亮，我们买了十朵红色的花。一共三百五十块。
Hòu lái wǒ men qù le huā diàn Diàn lǐ de huā hěn piào liàng wǒ men mǎi le shí duǒ hóng sè de huā Yí gòng sān bǎi wǔ shí kuài
Later we went to a flower shop. The flowers were beautiful, and we bought ten red ones. Three hundred and fifty dollars in all.
妹妹还画了一张卡片。我们希望妈妈会喜欢。
Mèi mei hái huà le yì zhāng kǎ piàn Wǒ men xī wàng mā ma huì xǐ huān
My sister also drew a card. We hope Mom will like it.

生詞

礼物 lǐwù　present, gift
百货公司 bǎihuògōngsī　department store
贵 guì　expensive
件 jiàn　measure word for clothes and matters
后来 hòulái　later, afterwards
漂亮 piàoliàng　pretty, beautiful
一共 yígòng　altogether, in all
希望 xīwàng　to hope
