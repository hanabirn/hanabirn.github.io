坐火車去台南

By train to Tainan — Chinese reading, TW HSK 1–2

文章

放假的時候，我坐火車去台南看朋友。從台北到台南要四個小時。
Fàng jià de shí hòu wǒ zuò huǒ chē qù Tái nán kàn péng yǒu Cóng Tái běi dào Tái nán yào sì ge xiǎo shí
During the holidays I took the train to Tainan to see a friend. From Taipei to Tainan takes four hours.
在火車上，我看書，也看窗戶外面的山。
Zài huǒ chē shàng wǒ kàn shū yě kàn chuāng hù wài miàn de shān
On the train I read, and I looked at the mountains outside the window.
朋友在車站等我。她帶我去喝牛肉湯，還去看了很老的廟。
Péng yǒu zài chē zhàn děng wǒ Tā dài wǒ qù hē niú ròu tāng hái qù kàn le hěn lǎo de miào
My friend was waiting for me at the station. She took me to have beef soup, and we also went to see a very old temple.
台南的天氣很熱，可是東西很好吃。明年我還想再去。
Tái nán de tiān qì hěn rè kě shì dōng xi hěn hǎo chī Míng nián wǒ hái xiǎng zài qù
The weather in Tainan is very hot, but the food is delicious. I want to go again next year.

生詞

火車 huǒchē　train
從 cóng　from
小時 xiǎoshí　hour
車站 chēzhàn　station
帶 dài　to take (someone somewhere)
熱 rè　hot
明年 míngnián　next year
