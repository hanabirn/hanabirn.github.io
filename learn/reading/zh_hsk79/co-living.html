“青年社区”的兴起

The rise of "youth co-living" — Chinese reading, HSK 7–9

文章

近年来，一些城市出现了面向年轻人的“青年社区”：每个人拥有独立的卧室，厨房、健身房和图书角则由住户共享，社区还定期组织读书会、手工课等活动。
jìn nián lái yì xiē chéng shì chū xiàn le miàn xiàng nián qīng rén de qīng nián shè qū měi gè rén yōng yǒu dú lì de wò shì chú fáng jiàn shēn fáng hé tú shū jiǎo zé yóu zhù hù gòng xiǎng shè qū hái dìng qī zǔ zhī dú shū huì shǒu gōng kè děng huó dòng
In recent years "youth communities" aimed at young people have appeared in some cities: everyone has their own bedroom, while the kitchen, gym and reading corner are shared, and the community regularly organises book clubs, craft classes and other activities.
这种模式之所以受到追捧，与其说是因为租金实惠，不如说是迎合了年轻人对归属感的渴望。背井离乡来到大城市打拼，不少人下班回到家，面对的只是四面白墙，孤独感油然而生。
zhè zhǒng mó shì zhī suǒ yǐ shòu dào zhuī pěng yǔ qí shuō shì yīn wèi zū jīn shí huì bù rú shuō shì yíng hé le nián qīng rén duì guī shǔ gǎn de kě wàng bèi jǐng lí xiāng lái dào dà chéng shì dǎ pīn bù shǎo rén xià bān huí dào jiā miàn duì de zhǐ shì sì miàn bái qiáng gū dú gǎn yóu rán ér shēng
This model is sought after not so much because the rent is good value as because it caters to young people's longing to belong. Having left home to strive in a big city, many face nothing but four white walls after work, and loneliness wells up.
当然，共享空间也难免带来摩擦，作息不同、卫生习惯各异，都可能引发矛盾。如何在私密与交往之间找到平衡，是这类社区能否长期运营下去的关键。
dāng rán gòng xiǎng kōng jiān yě nán miǎn dài lái mó cā zuò xī bù tóng wèi shēng xí guàn gè yì dōu kě néng yǐn fā máo dùn rú hé zài sī mì yǔ jiāo wǎng zhī jiān zhǎo dào píng héng shì zhè lèi shè qū néng fǒu cháng qī yùn yíng xià qù de guān jiàn
Of course shared space inevitably brings friction: different schedules and hygiene habits can all cause conflict. How to strike a balance between privacy and socialising is the key to whether such communities can keep running in the long term.
