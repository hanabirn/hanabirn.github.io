登山熱潮

The hiking boom — Chinese reading, HSK 6

文章

近年來，臺灣掀起了一股登山熱潮，政府也開放許多過去需要申請的山林，鼓勵民眾親近大自然。假日的步道上，常常可以看到全家大小一起出遊。
jìn nián lái Tái wān xiān qǐ le yì gǔ dēng shān rè cháo zhèng fǔ yě kāi fàng xǔ duō guò qù xū yào shēn qǐng de shān lín gǔ lì mín zhòng qīn jìn dà zì rán jià rì de bù dào shàng cháng cháng kě yǐ kàn dào quán jiā dà xiǎo yì qǐ chū yóu
In recent years Taiwan has seen a hiking boom, and the government has opened up many mountain areas that used to require permits, encouraging people to get close to nature. On holiday trails you often see whole families out together.
然而，登山人口快速增加，山難事件也隨之增多。有些人準備不足，穿著拖鞋就上山；有些人為了拍照，擅自離開步道而迷路。每一次搜救，都要動用大量人力，甚至讓救難人員冒著生命危險。
rán ér dēng shān rén kǒu kuài sù zēng jiā shān nán shì jiàn yě suí zhī zēng duō yǒu xiē rén zhǔn bèi bù zú chuān zhe tuō xié jiù shàng shān yǒu xiē rén wèi le pāi zhào shàn zì lí kāi bù dào ér mí lù měi yí cì sōu jiù dōu yào dòng yòng dà liàng rén lì shèn zhì ràng jiù nán rén yuán mào zhe shēng mìng wéi xiǎn
But as the number of hikers has soared, mountain accidents have risen too. Some go unprepared, climbing in flip-flops; others leave the trail without permission for photos and get lost. Every search and rescue takes huge manpower and can even put rescuers' lives at risk.
山是開放的，責任卻要自己承擔。做好功課、量力而為，才是對大自然真正的尊重。
shān shì kāi fàng de zé rèn què yào zì jǐ chéng dān zuò hǎo gōng kè liàng lì ér wéi cái shì duì dà zì rán zhēn zhèng de zūn zhòng
The mountains are open, but the responsibility is your own. Doing your homework and knowing your limits is true respect for nature.

步道 bùdào　trail, footpath
山難 shānnán　mountain accident

承擔 chéngdān　to bear, shoulder
