The House Swap

交換房子

— English reading, B1

文章

Last summer, my family tried something unusual: we swapped homes with a family from Portugal for three weeks. We found them on a website that is used by thousands of families.
去年夏天，我們家試了一件不尋常的事：和一個葡萄牙家庭交換房子住了三個星期。我們是在一個有數千個家庭使用的網站上找到他們的。
Before they arrived, my mother was worried that they would break something. She hid our best plates and wrote a long list of rules.
他們來之前，我媽媽擔心他們會打破東西。她把最好的盤子藏了起來，還寫了一長串規則。
Their house, which was near the sea, was smaller than ours but full of light. They had left us fresh bread, local cheese and a map with their favorite beaches.
他們的房子靠近海邊，比我們家小，但充滿陽光。他們給我們留了新鮮的麵包、當地的起司，還有一張標著他們最愛海灘的地圖。
When we got home, everything was cleaner than before. My mother admitted that her list had been unnecessary. We are already planning our next swap.
我們回到家時，一切都比原來還乾淨。媽媽承認她那張清單根本沒必要。我們已經在計畫下一次交換了。
