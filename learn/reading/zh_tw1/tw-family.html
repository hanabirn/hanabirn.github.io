我家

My family — Chinese reading, HSK 1

文章

我家在臺北，家裡有四個人。爸爸是醫生，媽媽是老師。
wǒ jiā zài Tái běi jiā lǐ yǒu sì gè rén bà ba shì yī shēng mā ma shì lǎo shī
My home is in Taipei, and there are four people in my family. Dad is a doctor and Mom is a teacher.
我有一個妹妹，她今年六歲。我們家有一隻貓，牠很小，也很可愛。我很愛我的家。
wǒ yǒu yí gè mèi mei tā jīn nián liù suì wǒ men jiā yǒu yì zhī māo tā hěn xiǎo yě hěn kě ài wǒ hěn ài wǒ de jiā
I have a little sister; she is six this year. We have a cat — it's very small and very cute. I love my family.

生詞
