坐捷運

Taking the MRT — Chinese reading, HSK 1

文章

我每天坐捷運去公司。從我家到公司要坐五站，大概十五分鐘。
wǒ měi tiān zuò jié yùn qù gōng sī cóng wǒ jiā dào gōng sī yào zuò wǔ zhàn dà gài shí wǔ fēn zhōng
I take the MRT to work every day. From my home to the office is five stops, about fifteen minutes.
早上八點捷運上的人很多，常常沒有位子。所以我現在七點就出門。人少一點，我可以坐著看書。
zǎo shàng bā diǎn jié yùn shàng de rén hěn duō cháng cháng méi yǒu wèi zi suǒ yǐ wǒ xiàn zài qī diǎn jiù chū mén rén shǎo yì diǎn wǒ kě yǐ zuò zhe kàn shū
At eight in the morning the MRT is crowded and there's often no seat. So now I leave at seven. There are fewer people and I can sit and read.

生詞

捷運 jiéyùn　MRT, metro
公司 gōngsī　company, office
站 zhàn　station, stop
大概 dàgài　about, roughly
分鐘 fēnzhōng　minute
位子 wèizi　seat
出門 chūmén　to go out
坐 zuò　to sit; to ride
