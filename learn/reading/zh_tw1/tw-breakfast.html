早餐店

The breakfast shop — Chinese reading, HSK 1

文章

我家旁邊有一家早餐店。每天早上七點，我都去那裡吃早餐。
wǒ jiā páng biān yǒu yì jiā zǎo cān diàn měi tiān zǎo shàng qī diǎn wǒ dōu qù nà lǐ chī zǎo cān
There's a breakfast shop next to my home. At seven every morning I go there for breakfast.
我喜歡吃蛋餅，喝一杯熱豆漿。一共只要五十塊。老闆很好，每天都跟我說「早安」。
wǒ xǐ huān chī dàn bǐng hē yì bēi rè dòu jiāng yí gòng zhǐ yào wǔ shí kuài lǎo bǎn hěn hǎo měi tiān dōu gēn wǒ shuō zǎo ān
I like egg pancakes with a cup of hot soy milk. It's only fifty dollars altogether. The owner is very nice and says "good morning" to me every day.

生詞

早餐 zǎocān　breakfast

蛋餅 dànbǐng　egg pancake
杯 bēi　cup (measure word)
豆漿 dòujiāng　soy milk
一共 yígòng　altogether
老闆 lǎobǎn　owner, boss
