在图书馆

In the library — Chinese reading, HSK 3

文章

快要期末考试了，学校图书馆每天都坐满了学生。为了找到位子，我每天早上七点就去排队。
kuài yào qī mò kǎo shì le xué xiào tú shū guǎn měi tiān dōu zuò mǎn le xué sheng wèi le zhǎo dào wèi zi wǒ měi tiān zǎo shang qī diǎn jiù qù pái duì
Final exams are coming, and the university library is full of students every day. To get a seat, I queue from seven every morning.
图书馆里非常安静，大家都在认真地复习。有一次，我旁边的同学手机突然响了。声音很大，他的脸一下子就红了。
tú shū guǎn lǐ fēi cháng ān jìng dà jiā dōu zài rèn zhēn de fù xí yǒu yí cì wǒ páng biān de tóng xué shǒu jī tū rán xiǎng le shēng yīn hěn dà tā de liǎn yí xià zi jiù hóng le
The library is very quiet; everyone is revising hard. Once, the phone of the student next to me suddenly rang very loudly, and his face went red at once.
我觉得在图书馆比在宿舍学得好，因为宿舍里总是有人聊天或者玩游戏。不过每天起这么早，真的很累。
wǒ jué de zài tú shū guǎn bǐ zài sù shè xué de hǎo yīn wèi sù shè lǐ zǒng shì yǒu rén liáo tiān huò zhě wán yóu xì bú guò měi tiān qǐ zhè me zǎo zhēn de hěn lèi
I think I study better in the library than in the dorm, because someone in the dorm is always chatting or playing games. Still, getting up this early every day is really tiring.
