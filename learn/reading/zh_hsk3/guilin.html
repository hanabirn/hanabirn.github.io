桂林的山水

The hills and rivers of Guilin — Chinese reading, HSK 3

文章

大家都说桂林的山水是中国最美的。所以国庆节的时候，我和两个同事一起去了桂林。我们提前一个月就订好了酒店。
dà jiā dōu shuō guì lín de shān shuǐ shì Zhōng guó zuì měi de suǒ yǐ Guó qìng jié de shí hou wǒ hé liǎng gè tóng shì yì qǐ qù le guì lín wǒ men tí qián yí gè yuè jiù dìng hǎo le jiǔ diàn
Everyone says Guilin's scenery is the most beautiful in China, so over the National Day holiday I went to Guilin with two colleagues. We booked the hotel a month in advance.
到了以后才发现，那儿的游客比我们想的还要多。第二天我们坐船游江，两边的山像一幅画一样，非常漂亮。
dào le yǐ hòu cái fā xiàn nà r de yóu kè bǐ wǒ men xiǎng de hái yào duō dì èr tiān wǒ men zuò chuán yóu jiāng liǎng biān de shān xiàng yì fú huà yí yàng fēi cháng piào liang
Only after arriving did we find there were even more tourists than we had expected. On the second day we took a boat along the river; the hills on both sides looked like a painting.
虽然有点儿累，但是这次旅游让我很满意。下次我想换一个季节再去，那时候人应该会少一点儿。
suī rán yǒu diǎn r lèi dàn shì zhè cì lǚ yóu ràng wǒ hěn mǎn yì xià cì wǒ xiǎng huàn yí gè jì jié zài qù nà shí hou rén yīng gāi huì shǎo yì diǎn r
Although it was a little tiring, I was very pleased with the trip. Next time I'd like to go in a different season, when there should be fewer people.
