超市打折

A supermarket sale — Chinese reading, HSK 3

文章

我家楼下新开了一家超市。刚开的第一个星期，所有的水果和饮料都打八折，鸡蛋还买一送一。
wǒ jiā lóu xià xīn kāi le yì jiā chāo shì gāng kāi de dì yī gè xīng qī suǒ yǒu de shuǐ guǒ hé yǐn liào dōu dǎ bā zhé jī dàn hái mǎi yí sòng yī
A new supermarket opened downstairs from my home. In its first week all fruit and drinks were 20% off, and eggs were buy one, get one free.
星期天早上，我八点就去了。没想到门口已经排了很长的队，大部分是老人。我等了二十多分钟才进去。
xīng qī tiān zǎo shang wǒ bā diǎn jiù qù le méi xiǎng dào mén kǒu yǐ jīng pái le hěn cháng de duì dà bù fen shì lǎo rén wǒ děng le èr shí duō fēn zhōng cái jìn qù
On Sunday morning I went at eight, but to my surprise there was already a long queue at the door, mostly elderly people. I waited over twenty minutes to get in.
那天我买了很多东西，回家以后才发现，有一半其实用不上。妈妈说，便宜的东西不一定是需要的东西。
nà tiān wǒ mǎi le hěn duō dōng xi huí jiā yǐ hòu cái fā xiàn yǒu yí bàn qí shí yòng bú shàng mā ma shuō pián yi de dōng xi bù yí dìng shì xū yào de dōng xi
I bought a lot that day, and only after getting home did I realise half of it I'd never use. My mother said cheap things aren't necessarily things you need.

生詞

超市 chāoshì　supermarket
打折 dǎzhé　to give a discount
饮料 yǐnliào　drink, beverage
买一送一 mǎiyísòngyī　buy one, get one free
没想到 méixiǎngdào　unexpectedly
大部分 dàbùfen　the majority
其实 qíshí　actually
一定 yídìng　certainly (不一定: not necessarily)
