外送平臺

Food delivery apps — Chinese reading, HSK 5

文章

這幾年，臺灣的街上到處都是穿著粉紅色或綠色外套的外送員。只要在手機上點一點，從珍珠奶茶到牛肉麵，二十分鐘就能送到家。
zhè jǐ nián Tái wān de jiē shàng dào chù dōu shì chuān zhe fěn hóng sè huò lǜ sè wài tào de wài sòng yuán zhǐ yào zài shǒu jī shàng diǎn yì diǎn cóng zhēn zhū nǎi chá dào niú ròu miàn èr shí fēn zhōng jiù néng sòng dào jiā
In recent years Taiwan's streets have been full of delivery riders in pink or green jackets. Just a few taps on your phone, and anything from bubble tea to beef noodles arrives in twenty minutes.
外送確實方便，尤其是下大雨或工作忙的時候。但是外送費加上平臺抽成，同樣一碗麵往往要多付三、四十塊。另外，外送員為了趕時間，騎車常常很快，交通事故也跟著增加。
wài sòng què shí fāng biàn yóu qí shì xià dà yǔ huò gōng zuò máng de shí hòu dàn shì wài sòng fèi jiā shàng píng tái chōu chéng tóng yàng yì wǎn miàn wǎng wǎng yào duō fù sān sì shí kuài lìng wài wài sòng yuán wèi le gǎn shí jiān qí chē cháng cháng hěn kuài jiāo tōng shì gù yě gēn zhe zēng jiā
Delivery is certainly convenient, especially in heavy rain or when you're busy. But with the delivery fee and the platform's commission, the same bowl of noodles often costs thirty or forty dollars more. And riders racing against the clock often ride fast, so traffic accidents have risen too.
我認為，偶爾叫外送沒關係，但是如果走路五分鐘就到，還是自己去買吧。
wǒ rèn wéi ǒu ěr jiào wài sòng méi guān xi dàn shì rú guǒ zǒu lù wǔ fēn zhōng jiù dào hái shì zì jǐ qù mǎi ba
In my view, ordering delivery now and then is fine, but if it's five minutes' walk away, you might as well go yourself.
