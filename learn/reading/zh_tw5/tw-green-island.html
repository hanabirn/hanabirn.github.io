綠島浮潛

Snorkelling on Green Island — Chinese reading, HSK 5

文章

今年夏天，我第一次去綠島。從臺東坐船過去要五十分鐘，船搖得很厲害，不少人一下船就吐了。不過，看到清澈得像玻璃一樣的海水，大家馬上又有了精神。
jīn nián xià tiān wǒ dì yī cì qù lǜ dǎo cóng Tái dōng zuò chuán guò qù yào wǔ shí fēn zhōng chuán yáo de hěn lì hài bù shǎo rén yí xià chuán jiù tǔ le bú guò kàn dào qīng chè de xiàng bō lí yí yàng de hǎi shuǐ dà jiā mǎ shàng yòu yǒu le jīng shén
This summer I went to Green Island for the first time. The boat from Taitung takes fifty minutes and rocks so badly that quite a few people threw up as soon as they got off. But seeing the sea, clear as glass, everyone perked up at once.
第二天，教練帶我們去浮潛。我把臉放進水裡，五顏六色的魚就在身邊游來游去，還看到了一隻大海龜。
dì èr tiān jiào liàn dài wǒ men qù fú qián wǒ bǎ liǎn fàng jìn shuǐ lǐ wǔ yán liù sè de yú jiù zài shēn biān yóu lái yóu qù hái kàn dào le yì zhī dà hǎi guī
On the second day an instructor took us snorkelling. When I put my face in the water, fish of every colour swam all around me, and I even saw a big sea turtle.
教練提醒我們，千萬不能踩珊瑚，也不能餵魚。他說，這片海很美，但也很脆弱，需要每一位遊客一起保護。
jiào liàn tí xǐng wǒ men qiān wàn bù néng cǎi shān hú yě bù néng wèi yú tā shuō zhè piàn hǎi hěn měi dàn yě hěn cuì ruò xū yào měi yí wèi yóu kè yì qǐ bǎo hù
The instructor warned us never to step on the coral or feed the fish. He said this sea is beautiful but fragile, and needs every visitor to help protect it.

生詞

吐 tǔ　to vomit
清澈 qīngchè　crystal clear
精神 jīngshén　energy, spirits
浮潛 fúqián　to snorkel
五顏六色 wǔyánliùsè　multicoloured
珊瑚 shānhú　coral
脆弱 cuìruò　fragile
