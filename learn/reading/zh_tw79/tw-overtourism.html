網美景點

Instagram hotspots — Chinese reading, HSK 7–9

文章

社群媒體的興起，讓許多原本鮮為人知的角落一夕爆紅。一片花海、一座彩繪村，只要被網紅拍成美照分享，週末便湧入成千上萬的遊客。當地居民一方面樂見商機，另一方面卻不堪其擾：垃圾遍地、停車位一位難求，寧靜的生活蕩然無存。
shè qún méi tǐ de xīng qǐ ràng xǔ duō yuán běn xiǎn wéi rén zhī de jiǎo luò yì xī bào hóng yí piàn huā hǎi yí zuò cǎi huì cūn zhǐ yào bèi wǎng hóng pāi chéng měi zhào fēn xiǎng zhōu mò biàn yǒng rù chéng qiān shàng wàn de yóu kè dāng dì jū mín yì fāng miàn lè jiàn shāng jī lìng yì fāng miàn què bù kān qí rǎo lè sè biàn dì tíng chē wèi yí wèi nán qiú níng jìng de shēng huó dàng rán wú cún
The rise of social media has made many once little-known spots go viral overnight. A sea of flowers or a painted village needs only a beautiful photo from an influencer, and tens of thousands of visitors pour in at weekends. Locals welcome the business but are also overwhelmed: rubbish everywhere, parking impossible, and their quiet life gone without trace.
更令人遺憾的是，有些景點在熱潮退去後迅速沒落，留下一地荒涼。
gèng lìng rén yí hàn de shì yǒu xiē jǐng diǎn zài rè cháo tuì qù hòu xùn sù mò luò liú xià yí dì huāng liáng
More regrettably still, some spots decline rapidly once the craze passes, leaving desolation behind.
旅遊的意義，不該只是打卡拍照，而是放慢腳步，真正認識一個地方的人文與自然。
lǚ yóu de yì yì bù gāi zhǐ shì dǎ kǎ pāi zhào ér shì fàng màn jiǎo bù zhēn zhèng rèn shì yí gè dì fāng de rén wén yǔ zì rán
The point of travel shouldn't be just checking in and taking photos, but slowing down and truly getting to know a place's culture and nature.
