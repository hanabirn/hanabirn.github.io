社會住宅

Social housing — Chinese reading, HSK 7–9

文章

面對居高不下的房價，政府近年積極興建社會住宅，以低於市價的租金，提供給青年、弱勢家庭及在地就業者。這項政策立意良善，卻常在選址階段就遭遇阻力。
miàn duì jū gāo bú xià de fáng jià zhèng fǔ jìn nián jī jí xīng jiàn shè huì zhù zhái yǐ dī yú shì jià de zū jīn tí gōng gěi qīng nián ruò shì jiā tíng jí zài dì jiù yè zhě zhè xiàng zhèng cè lì yì liáng shàn què cháng zài xuǎn zhǐ jiē duàn jiù zāo yù zǔ lì
Faced with persistently high house prices, the government has been actively building social housing in recent years, renting it below market rates to young people, disadvantaged families and local workers. The policy is well-intentioned, yet often meets resistance at the site-selection stage.
部分居民擔憂社宅進駐會拉低周邊房價、影響治安，於是群起抗議，形成所謂的「鄰避效應」。然而，國外經驗顯示，規劃完善的社宅不但不會使社區沒落，反而能帶來托育、長照等公共設施，讓整個街區受惠。
bù fèn jū mín dān yōu shè zhái jìn zhù huì lā dī zhōu biān fáng jià yǐng xiǎng zhì ān yú shì qún qǐ kàng yì xíng chéng suǒ wèi de lín bì xiào yìng rán ér guó wài jīng yàn xiǎn shì guī huà wán shàn de shè zhái bú dàn bú huì shǐ shè qū mò luò fǎn ér néng dài lái tuō yù cháng zhào děng gōng gòng shè shī ràng zhěng gè jiē qū shòu huì
Some residents fear social housing will drag down nearby prices and affect public order, and protest en masse — the so-called NIMBY effect. Yet experience abroad shows that well-planned social housing doesn't cause decline; rather it brings childcare, elderly care and other public facilities that benefit the whole district.
化解疑慮的關鍵，在於政府與居民之間能否建立起真誠的溝通與信任。
huà jiě yí lǜ de guān jiàn zài yú zhèng fǔ yǔ jū mín zhī jiān néng fǒu jiàn lì qǐ zhēn chéng de gōu tōng yǔ xìn rèn
The key to dispelling doubts lies in whether genuine communication and trust can be built between the government and residents.
