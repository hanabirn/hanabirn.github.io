健康檢查

A health check-up — Chinese reading, HSK 3

文章

我爸爸今年五十歲，公司每年都讓員工去醫院做健康檢查。上個月的報告出來以後，醫生說他的血壓有點高，體重也太重了。
wǒ bà ba jīn nián wǔ shí suì gōng sī měi nián dōu ràng yuán gōng qù yī yuàn zuò jiàn kāng jiǎn chá shàng gè yuè de bào gào chū lái yǐ hòu yī shēng shuō tā de xuè yā yǒu diǎn gāo tǐ zhòng yě tài zhòng le
My dad is fifty this year, and his company sends staff for a hospital check-up every year. When last month's report came out, the doctor said his blood pressure was a bit high and he was overweight.
從那天開始，媽媽做菜少放鹽，也不再買飲料和零食。爸爸每天晚上吃完飯都去河邊散步一個小時，週末還去爬山。三個月以後，他瘦了五公斤，血壓也正常了。
cóng nà tiān kāi shǐ mā ma zuò cài shǎo fàng yán yě bú zài mǎi yǐn liào hé líng shí bà ba měi tiān wǎn shàng chī wán fàn dōu qù hé biān sàn bù yí gè xiǎo shí zhōu mò hái qù pá shān sān gè yuè yǐ hòu tā shòu le wǔ gōng jīn xuè yā yě zhèng cháng le
From that day on, Mom used less salt in her cooking and stopped buying drinks and snacks. Every evening after dinner Dad walks by the river for an hour, and at weekends he goes hiking. Three months later he had lost five kilos and his blood pressure was normal.
