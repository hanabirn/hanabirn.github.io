騎YouBike

Riding YouBike — Chinese reading, HSK 3

文章

臺北街上到處都能看到黃色的YouBike。只要用悠遊卡一刷，就可以把車騎走，騎到別的站再還車。
Tái běi jiē shàng dào chù dōu néng kàn dào huáng sè de zhǐ yào yòng yōu yóu kǎ yì shuā jiù kě yǐ bǎ chē qí zǒu qí dào bié de zhàn zài huán chē
Yellow YouBikes are everywhere on Taipei's streets. Just tap an EasyCard and you can ride one away, then return it at another station.
我住的地方離捷運站有點遠，走路要十五分鐘。自從有了YouBike，我每天早上都騎車去捷運站，五分鐘就到了。
wǒ zhù de dì fāng lí jié yùn zhàn yǒu diǎn yuǎn zǒu lù yào shí wǔ fēn zhōng zì cóng yǒu le wǒ měi tiān zǎo shàng dōu qí chē qù jié yùn zhàn wǔ fēn zhōng jiù dào le
Where I live is a bit far from the MRT station — fifteen minutes on foot. Since YouBike came along I ride to the station every morning, and it takes five minutes.
不過下班的時候，捷運站旁邊的車常常被借光了，有時候要等好幾分鐘才有空位還車。
bú guò xià bān de shí hòu jié yùn zhàn páng biān de chē cháng cháng bèi jiè guāng le yǒu shí hòu yào děng hǎo jǐ fēn zhōng cái yǒu kòng wèi huán chē
But at the end of the working day the bikes by the station have often all been taken, and sometimes you have to wait several minutes for a free dock to return one.

悠遊卡 yōuyóukǎ　EasyCard (transit card)
刷 shuā　to swipe, tap
還車 huánchē　to return (a bike)
有點 yǒudiǎn　a little
自從 zìcóng　since
借 jiè　to borrow
空位 kòngwèi　vacant spot
