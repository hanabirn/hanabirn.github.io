補習班

Cram school — Chinese reading, HSK 3

文章

我讀國中的時候，每天放學以後還要去補習班上課。補習班在火車站附近，常常上到晚上九點才下課。回家吃完晚餐，還要寫學校的作業，常常十二點才睡覺。
wǒ dú guó zhōng de shí hòu měi tiān fàng xué yǐ hòu hái yào qù bǔ xí bān shàng kè bǔ xí bān zài huǒ chē zhàn fù jìn cháng cháng shàng dào wǎn shàng jiǔ diǎn cái xià kè huí jiā chī wán wǎn cān hái yào xiě xué xiào de zuò yè cháng cháng shí èr diǎn cái shuì jiào
In junior high, I had to go to cram school every day after school. It was near the train station, and classes often ran until nine at night. After dinner at home I still had school homework, and often only got to bed at midnight.
那時候我覺得很累，也不太明白為什麼要這麼辛苦。現在上了大學，我才發現，補習班的老師教我的不只是考試的方法，還有怎麼安排時間。
nà shí hòu wǒ jué de hěn lèi yě bú tài míng bái wèi shén me yào zhè me xīn kǔ xiàn zài shàng le dà xué wǒ cái fā xiàn bǔ xí bān de lǎo shī jiāo wǒ de bù zhǐ shì kǎo shì de fāng fǎ hái yǒu zěn me ān pái shí jiān
At the time I was exhausted and didn't really understand why it had to be so hard. Now that I'm at university, I've realised that what the cram-school teachers taught me wasn't just exam techniques but also how to organise my time.
