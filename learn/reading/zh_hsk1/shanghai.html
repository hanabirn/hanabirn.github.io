去上海

Going to Shanghai — Chinese reading, HSK 1

文章

上个月，我和朋友坐火车去了上海。火车很快，我们坐了五个小时。
shàng gè yuè wǒ hé péng you zuò huǒ chē qù le Shàng hǎi huǒ chē hěn kuài wǒ men zuò le wǔ gè xiǎo shí
Last month my friend and I went to Shanghai by train. The train was fast; we rode for five hours.
上海很大，人也很多。我们住在一个小饭店里，饭店离火车站不远。
Shàng hǎi hěn dà rén yě hěn duō wǒ men zhù zài yí gè xiǎo fàn diàn lǐ fàn diàn lí huǒ chē zhàn bù yuǎn
Shanghai is big and there are lots of people. We stayed in a small hotel not far from the station.
我们去了很多商店，也买了很多东西。我很想明年再去。
wǒ men qù le hěn duō shāng diàn yě mǎi le hěn duō dōng xi wǒ hěn xiǎng míng nián zài qù
We went to many shops and bought lots of things too. I really want to go again next year.

生詞

上个月 shànggèyuè　last month
火车站 huǒchēzhàn　railway station
火车 huǒchē　train
小时 xiǎoshí　hour
饭店 fàndiàn　hotel, restaurant
离 lí　away from
东西 dōngxi　thing(s)
明年 míngnián　next year
