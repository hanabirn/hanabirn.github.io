我的家

My home — Chinese reading, HSK 1

文章

我家在北京。我家有四口人：爸爸、妈妈、哥哥和我。
wǒ jiā zài Běi jīng wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén bà ba mā ma gē ge hé wǒ
My home is in Beijing. There are four people in my family: Dad, Mum, my older brother and me.
我们的家不大，但是很好。家里有三个房间。我的房间里有一张床和一张桌子。
wǒ men de jiā bú dà dàn shì hěn hǎo jiā li yǒu sān gè fáng jiān wǒ de fáng jiān lǐ yǒu yì zhāng chuáng hé yì zhāng zhuō zi
Our home isn't big, but it's nice. There are three rooms. In my room there is a bed and a table.
晚上，我们一起吃饭、看电视。我很喜欢我的家。
wǎn shang wǒ men yì qǐ chī fàn kàn diàn shì wǒ hěn xǐ huan wǒ de jiā
In the evening we eat and watch TV together. I really like my home.

生詞

口 kǒu　measure word for family members
哥哥 gēge　older brother
但是 dànshì　but
房间 fángjiān　room
张 Zhāng　measure word for flat things
床 chuáng　bed
桌子 zhuōzi　table
一起 yìqǐ　together
