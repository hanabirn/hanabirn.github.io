慢旅行

Slow travel — Chinese reading, HSK 5

文章

很多人旅游喜欢“打卡”：一天跑五六个景点，每到一个地方拍几张照片就赶往下一个。回家以后看照片，却想不起来那些地方有什么特别。
hěn duō rén lǚ yóu xǐ huan dǎ kǎ yì tiān pǎo wǔ liù gè jǐng diǎn měi dào yí gè dì fang pāi jǐ zhāng zhào piàn jiù gǎn wǎng xià yí gè huí jiā yǐ hòu kàn zhào piàn què xiǎng bù qi lai nà xiē dì fang yǒu shén me tè bié
Many people like to "check in" when travelling: rushing round five or six sights a day, snapping a few photos at each before hurrying on to the next. Back home they look at the photos but can't recall what was special about those places.
去年秋天，我试了一次“慢旅行”。我在云南的一个小镇住了整整一周，每天只做一两件事：在老街散步，去市场看当地人买菜，或者在河边坐一个下午。
qù nián qiū tiān wǒ shì le yí cì màn lǚ xíng wǒ zài Yún nán de yí gè xiǎo zhèn zhù le zhěng zhěng yì zhōu měi tiān zhǐ zuò yì liǎng jiàn shì zài lǎo jiē sàn bù qù shì chǎng kàn dāng dì rén mǎi cài huò zhě zài hé biān zuò yí gè xià wǔ
Last autumn I tried "slow travel". I stayed a whole week in a small town in Yunnan and did only one or two things a day: walking the old streets, watching locals shop at the market, or sitting by the river for an afternoon.
这样的旅行看起来没什么收获，但我却第一次真正了解了一个地方的生活节奏。我想，旅行的意义不在于去了多少地方，而在于有没有用心感受。
zhè yàng de lǚ xíng kàn qi lai méi shén me shōu huò dàn wǒ què dì yī cì zhēn zhèng liǎo jiě le yí gè dì fang de shēng huó jié zòu wǒ xiǎng lǚ xíng de yì yì bú zài yú qù le duō shǎo dì fang ér zài yú yǒu méi yǒu yòng xīn gǎn shòu
Such a trip might look like it achieved nothing, but for the first time I truly got to know a place's rhythm of life. I think the point of travelling lies not in how many places you go, but in whether you take the time to really feel them.

生詞

景点 jǐngdiǎn　tourist sight
赶 gǎn　to hurry
小镇 xiǎozhèn　small town
整整 zhěngzhěng　whole, fully
收获 shōuhuò　gain, results
节奏 jiézòu　rhythm, pace
意义 yìyì　meaning, significance
在于 zàiyú　to lie in
