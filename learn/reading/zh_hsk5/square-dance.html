广场舞

Square dancing — Chinese reading, HSK 5

文章

每天傍晚，我家附近的广场上都有几十位阿姨跟着音乐跳舞。她们大多已经退休，跳广场舞既能锻炼身体，又能认识新朋友。我妈妈参加以后，不但瘦了五公斤，心情也开朗了很多。
měi tiān bàng wǎn wǒ jiā fù jìn de guǎng chǎng shàng dōu yǒu jǐ shí wèi ā yí gēn zhe yīn yuè tiào wǔ tā men dà duō yǐ jīng tuì xiū tiào guǎng chǎng wǔ jì néng duàn liàn shēn tǐ yòu néng rèn shi xīn péng you wǒ mā ma cān jiā yǐ hòu bú dàn shòu le wǔ gōng jīn xīn qíng yě kāi lǎng le hěn duō
Every evening dozens of women dance to music in the square near my home. Most are retired; square dancing lets them exercise and make new friends. Since my mum joined she has lost five kilos and become much more cheerful.
不过，广场舞也引起过不少矛盾。音乐声音太大，影响了附近居民休息，尤其是要上早班的年轻人和刚出生的婴儿。
bú guò guǎng chǎng wǔ yě yǐn qǐ guo bù shǎo máo dùn yīn yuè shēng yīn tài dà yǐng xiǎng le fù jìn jū mín xiū xi yóu qí shì yào shàng zǎo bān de nián qīng rén hé gāng chū shēng de yīng ér
But square dancing has also caused plenty of friction. The music is too loud and disturbs nearby residents' rest — especially young people on early shifts and newborn babies.
后来，社区和跳舞的阿姨们商量好：音量调小，晚上八点半以前结束。问题解决了，大家的关系也更和谐了。可见，只要互相理解，矛盾是可以化解的。
hòu lái shè qū hé tiào wǔ de ā yí men shāng liang hǎo yīn liàng diào xiǎo wǎn shang bā diǎn bàn yǐ qián jié shù wèn tí jiě jué le dà jiā de guān xi yě gèng hé xié le kě jiàn zhǐ yào hù xiāng lǐ jiě máo dùn shì kě yǐ huà jiě de
Later the neighbourhood committee and the dancers agreed to turn the volume down and finish before half past eight. The problem was solved, and relations got friendlier. Clearly, as long as people understand each other, conflicts can be resolved.

生詞

傍晚 bàngwǎn　dusk, early evening
广场 guǎngchǎng　square, plaza
退休 tuìxiū　to retire
开朗 kāilǎng　cheerful
矛盾 máodùn　conflict, friction
居民 jūmín　resident
社区 shèqū　community
和谐 héxié　harmonious
