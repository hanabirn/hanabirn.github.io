直播带货

Live-stream selling — Chinese reading, HSK 5

文章

这两年，直播带货越来越流行。主播在镜头前介绍商品，一边试用一边讲解，观众看中了就能马上下单，价格往往比商店还便宜。
zhè liǎng nián zhí bō dài huò yuè lái yuè liú xíng zhǔ bō zài jìng tóu qián jiè shào shāng pǐn yì biān shì yòng yì biān jiǎng jiě guān zhòng kàn zhòng le jiù néng mǎ shàng xià dān jià gé wǎng wǎng bǐ shāng diàn hái pián yi
Live-stream selling has become more and more popular these past two years. Hosts introduce products on camera, trying them out while explaining, and viewers who like something can order on the spot, often at prices lower than in shops.
我妈妈就是一位忠实的观众。她说看直播像跟朋友聊天，很有意思。可是上个月，她买的一件羽绒服到货后发现质量很差，跟直播里看到的完全不一样，退货也花了好几天。
wǒ mā ma jiù shì yí wèi zhōng shí de guān zhòng tā shuō kàn zhí bō xiàng gēn péng you liáo tiān hěn yǒu yì si kě shì shàng gè yuè tā mǎi de yí jiàn yǔ róng fú dào huò hòu fā xiàn zhì liàng hěn chà gēn zhí bō lǐ kàn dào de wán quán bù yí yàng tuì huò yě huā le hǎo jǐ tiān
My mum is a loyal viewer. She says watching is like chatting with friends — great fun. But last month a down jacket she bought turned out to be poor quality, nothing like what she saw on the stream, and returning it took days.
直播购物方便又有趣，但是主播的话不能全信。买之前最好看看其他买家的评价，冷静地想一想自己是不是真的需要。
zhí bō gòu wù fāng biàn yòu yǒu qù dàn shì zhǔ bō de huà bù néng quán xìn mǎi zhī qián zuì hǎo kàn kan qí tā mǎi jiā de píng jià lěng jìng de xiǎng yì xiǎng zì jǐ shì bú shì zhēn de xū yào
Live-stream shopping is convenient and fun, but you can't believe everything the hosts say. Before buying, it's best to read other buyers' reviews and think calmly about whether you really need it.

生詞

直播 zhíbō　live stream
主播 zhǔbō　stream host
商品 shāngpǐn　goods, product
往往 wǎngwǎng　often, usually
忠实 zhōngshí　loyal
质量 zhìliàng　quality
退货 tuìhuò　to return goods
评价 píngjià　review, evaluation
