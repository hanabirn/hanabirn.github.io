景点的烦恼

The trouble with tourist hotspots — Chinese reading, HSK 6

文章

每逢长假，各大景点总是人山人海。有网友调侃说：“到了长城，看到的不是城墙，而是人的后脑勺。”游客花了钱、受了累，却难以获得应有的体验。
měi féng cháng jià gè dà jǐng diǎn zǒng shì rén shān rén hǎi yǒu wǎng yǒu tiáo kǎn shuō dào le Cháng chéng kàn dào de bú shì chéng qiáng ér shì rén de hòu nǎo sháo yóu kè huā le qián shòu le lèi què nán yǐ huò dé yīng yǒu de tǐ yàn
Every long holiday the big sights are packed with people. One netizen joked: "At the Great Wall, what you see isn't the wall but the backs of people's heads." Tourists spend money and wear themselves out, yet hardly get the experience they deserve.
人太多不仅影响旅游质量，还会对景区造成损害：古建筑被摸得发亮，草地被踩成了泥地，垃圾随处可见。
rén tài duō bù jǐn yǐng xiǎng lǚ yóu zhì liàng hái huì duì jǐng qū zào chéng sǔn hài gǔ jiàn zhù bèi mō de fā liàng cǎo dì bèi cǎi chéng le ní dì lā jī suí chù kě jiàn
Too many people not only spoil the trip but also damage the sites: old buildings get rubbed shiny, lawns are trampled into mud, and litter is everywhere.
为此，不少景区开始实行预约制度，限制每天的游客数量。起初有人抱怨不方便，但事实证明，这一做法既保护了景区，也让游客玩得更舒心。旅游业要想长期发展，就不能只看眼前的门票收入。
wèi cǐ bù shǎo jǐng qū kāi shǐ shí xíng yù yuē zhì dù xiàn zhì měi tiān de yóu kè shù liàng qǐ chū yǒu rén bào yuàn bù fāng biàn dàn shì shí zhèng míng zhè yí zuò fǎ jì bǎo hù le jǐng qū yě ràng yóu kè wán de gèng shū xīn lǚ yóu yè yào xiǎng cháng qī fā zhǎn jiù bù néng zhǐ kàn yǎn qián de mén piào shōu rù
So many sites have introduced booking systems that cap daily visitor numbers. At first some complained it was inconvenient, but it has proved to both protect the sites and make visits more pleasant. If tourism is to thrive in the long run, it can't look only at today's ticket income.
