# Vocabulary quiz, spaced repetition, practice tools, home, learning path

(Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-24; keep it up to date when changing these files.)

**Vocabulary quiz (`js/quiz.js`, the largest file)**: the quiz engine. A pasted custom Google Sheet URL still goes through `loadSheetData` and the per-language `parseX(rows)` functions (fixed-width row "stride" parsing — see `parseJapanese`/`parseJapanese2`). Quiz state (mode, current word, score, shuffled order) lives in module-level `let` variables, not a framework state store. Scoring is +10 right, −3 wrong, floored at 0. Mistakes, quiz history, and flashcard "known" words persist to `localStorage` (`quiz_mistakes`, `quiz_records`, `flashcard_known`). Write progress with `storeSet(key, value)` (top of `js/quiz.js`), not a bare `localStorage.setItem`: when the ~5 MB quota is full it drops the re-downloadable `quiz_cache_*` word-list copies (biggest first, keeping the current set) until the write fits, so progress is never silently lost and a result screen never throws. **Half-done rounds (2026-10-22):** `resumeSave(key, state)` / `resumeGet` / `resumeClear` (also top of `js/quiz.js`) keep a reading passage's answers, a reading or listening practice round's answers and place in `study_resume` (synced; per key the newer `at` wins; a finished round becomes `{done, at}` rather than being deleted, so an older cloud copy can't bring it back; dropped after `RESUME_KEEP_DAYS`), keyed `reading|<level>:<passage>`, `reading|<level>:set:<set>`, `listening|<level>:<set>`; switchPage, a language change and `visibilitychange` save the one on screen. Mock exams are never kept and open on `mockNoteHtml()` (js/grammar.js) saying so, with 我知道了，開始. **Japanese reading options** are look-alike kana as in the JLPT (`generateSimilarReadings()` / `kanaSlips()`: a long vowel added, dropped or moved, っ, a dakuten, ゃゅょ; a changed vowel only for short words; `KANA_ALSO_RIGHT` keeps out another right reading such as にっぽん) — in the free quiz, path lessons, reviews and the listening tool; the Discord bot uses a copy, `worker/discord/src/kana.js`, with its seeded rand — keep the two alike. Every answer goes through one path in `selectOption()`: grade the SRS card, a miss → `addMistakeEntry()`, a hit → `noteMistakeCorrect()`. A mistake is **repaired** after `MISTAKE_FIX_STREAK` (2) right answers in a row (`entry.fix`; a miss or a timeout resets it), or by one right answer in 間隔複習 (`noteMistakeCorrect(id, true)`, since 2026-10-22: those are asked after a gap, and asked only once a session, so they could never complete a streak); it then gets `fixedAt`, stays in the book struck through for `MISTAKE_FIXED_KEEP_DAYS` (30) and returns to the list if missed again. Badges and home count only open ones (`getActiveMistakes()`). Per-session extras live there too: `comboNow`/`comboBest` ("連對"), `sessionStartedAt`, both reset when a session asks question 1. **Wrong options never mean the same as the answer or each other (2026-10-25):** `meaningsClash(a, b)` (a shared sense after splitting on ；;,，、/ and dropping parentheses, or a Chinese sense inside another) and `wordsClash(set, x, y)` — pairs from `data/vocab/clash.json` (`{set: [[word, word], …]}`, built by `tools/build_clash.py` with Gemini: synonyms no string test sees, 再見 / 拜拜; loaded once by `loadMeaningClash()` at `nextQuestion()`) — keep them apart in the review / lesson branch (filled up anyway when nothing else is left), the Chinese free practice and the meaning questions; readings, pinyin and zhuyin are left alone (`isMeaningGroup`). Show a quiz-page card with `showOnlyQuizCard(id)` (`QUIZ_PAGE_CARDS` lists them all) rather than toggling cards one by one. Score-card sharing renders a `<canvas>` and exports as an image.

**Spaced repetition (`srs_quiz`, in `js/quiz.js`)**: every answered quiz question (any word set, including JLPT/TOPIK/HSK) and every mistake-review answer grades a Leitner card via `gradeSrsQuizCard(entry, correct)` — right moves it up a box (intervals `SRS_INTERVAL_DAYS` = 0/1/3/7/14/30 days), wrong drops it to box 1 due in 10 minutes; a timer timeout counts as wrong. Since 2026-10-22 (owner's picks): a word answered right the first time starts at `SRS_FIRST_RIGHT_BOX` (4, due in 7 days — every right answer used to add a card due tomorrow), and a card answered right in the last box graduates (`grad`, due in `SRS_GRADUATED_DAYS`, still counted as 精通; a later miss brings it back to box 1; the "next batch" times skip graduated cards). The word 聽力測驗 grades cards too. Cards use the **same shape and id as mistake-book entries** (`quizEntryFor(type, answer)` → `{id: lang|word|group, lang, word, answer, hint, group, zct}`), which is what lets the review run on the mistake-review engine: `startSrsQuizReview()` sets `reviewMode = true` with `reviewSource = 'srs'` (vs `'mistakes'`) and up to `SRS_REVIEW_LIMIT` (20) due cards mixed across sets; `nextQuestion()`'s review branch fills wrong options from the same set's cards, then that set's `quiz_cache_<set>`, then near-miss kana for readings. The quiz page's "間隔複習" tool first opens the `#srs-card` page (due count, next batch, 7-day forecast, cards per stage; `startSrsQuizReview('tool')`, whose "返回" comes back there); the home dashboard starts the review directly and "返回" goes home. Anything that starts a new word set must clear the review state (`endReviewState()` in `selectWordSet`), because `showModeSelection()` treats a lingering `reviewMode` as "go to the mistake book". The old flashcard-only SRS (`srs_cards`) was removed in phase 2 of the 2026-10 redesign; its data is simply ignored.

**Home dashboard (`js/home.js` + `css/home.css`)**: `renderHome()` fills `#page-home` from data `js/quiz.js` already keeps in localStorage — `daily_activity` (`{'Y-M-D': words practised}`, bumped by `logDailyActivity()` on every answered quiz/listening question and every rated flashcard, pruned to 120 days), `calcStreak()` (a day counts if a quiz was finished *or* any word was practised), `last_quiz_set` (set by `startQuiz()`, drives "繼續學習"), `srs_cards`, `quiz_mistakes` and `quiz_records`. The only home-specific key is `daily_goal` (10/20/30/50, cycled by tapping the ring). **The 今天 card (`#home-today`, proposal B 2026-10-17)** replaced the goal card, the 繼續學習 button and the review / mistake cards: `homeTodaySteps()` lists, in order, the due SRS cards (a batch of `SRS_REVIEW_LIMIT`, only once the visitor has cards), the daily goal and — while any are open — the mistake book; done steps get a tick, and `#home-continue` (`homeTodayGo()`) goes to the first one not done (all done → keep practising the last set). Below it 繼續上次的 (`#home-recent`): the recent word sets plus the 文法 / 閱讀 / 聽解 level last opened in the learning language (`homeLearnChips()`, from `learn_last_levels`, fetching those pages' index.json when not loaded yet); 剛開始學？ (`#home-beginner`) only shows before any practice. The exam shortcuts were removed (the 單字 page's 檢定等級 tab). On computers (≥1024px, big check 2026-10-24) home is 1180px wide in two columns (css/home.css): the greeting across the top, 今天 left, the rest right; css/rails.css treats it as a wide page (the left rail needs ≥1700px, and the greeting keeps its small 小花火 while the rail is hidden). It re-renders on load, on opening the page and on language switch (`refreshDynamicContent`). To jump into the quiz page from home, `homeResetQuizPage()` clears `currentLang` *before* calling `backToLanguage()` — otherwise a lingering set or review sends 返回 elsewhere.

**Practice tools (`js/practice.js` + `css/practice.css`, redesigned 2026-10)**: the screens around the quiz engine. `renderPracticeHub()` draws the 練習中心 grid under the language buttons (`#quiz-tools`): a tile per tool with what's waiting (due SRS cards, open mistakes, known flashcards, last listening score, words this week) and a 小花火 suggestion for the most urgent; before the first answer of any kind (no SRS card, mistake, known flashcard, quiz record or `daily_activity`) the hub is hidden and `#lang-card.no-tools` gives the word sets the whole width (2026-10-25); it re-renders via `updateMistakeBadge()` and `refreshDynamicContent()`. The mistake book (`renderMistakeBook`, redesigned 2026-10-22) is a ruled notebook filtered in two steps — the language (`mbLang`, via `mbLangOf()`: flag, name, open mistakes), then that language's levels (`mbGroup`: a set id, all topic sets as `topics`, the notepad's as `notes`, the retired general sets as 舊版單字) — sorted by most-missed / recent / repaired, `MB_PAGE` (14) words a page in two columns on computers (the card is 1180px there: css/base.css, and `body.quiz-wide` for the rails; since the big check of 2026-10-24 the summary, repair button, filter and 清空 sit in a 300px side column `.mb-side` and a word's meaning shares its line with the word set, so a full page fits a 1080p screen — phones keep one column with 清空 last), red dots for times missed and two repair slots; "修復" (`startMistakeRepair`) runs up to 10 open mistakes on the review engine with `reviewSource = 'mistakes'`, re-asking each right answer once more a few questions later (`requeueForRepair`) so one round can repair a word. The stats page shows how much is learned (the best SRS box per word, grouped into 4 stages `SRS_STAGES`: 新學 1 / 學習中 2–3 / 熟悉 4 / 精通 5–6, per word set against its cached word count), a 16-week practice calendar from `daily_activity`, this week vs last week, achievements and recent quizzes. Flashcards: a deck list (`renderFlashcardDecks`, prefs in `flashcard_prefs`; on computers the options take a 340px column and the decks 3 a row) → a 20-card session (the set's 還不熟 words, then unseen ones in list order, topped up with known ones) on a card you flip by tap/space and rate by swiping (pointer events, `FC_SWIPE_PX`) or ← →. The result screen pieces (`resultHeroHtml`, `resultGoalHtml`, `resultHookHtml`) are shared by `showResults()` and the flashcard finish (on computers 小花火 sits beside the title, today's goal and the hook share a row and the buttons go in one row when they fit, so a lesson result fits a 1080p screen): confetti + 小花火, accuracy / best streak / time, the daily-goal ring, and a reason to come back (cards due now / within 24 h / next batch); "再練錯的字" (`retryWrongWords`) replays the session's misses as a repair round. The listening quiz (in `js/quiz.js`) has a one-page setup remembered in `listening_prefs`, a big play button with 🐢 slow (`speakText(…, {slow})`), and two ways to answer: `choice` (four look-alikes: near-miss kana for Japanese, same-first-letter words otherwise) or typing with letter boxes, a hint that reveals a character for 2 points, and a small-kana key row; a missed word goes to the mistake book as the entry `lessonEntryFor()` builds.

**Learning path (`js/path.js` + `css/path.css`)**: every built-in set (`QUIZ_LANG_ORDER`) opens on a path once loaded (`showSetStart()`; a pasted custom sheet still goes straight to the mode picker). The set is cut **in sheet row order** into levels of `LESSON_SIZE` (10) words, units of `LEVELS_PER_UNIT` (10) shown as `<details>`; inserting rows mid-sheet therefore shifts later words between levels. A level = word preview (`#lesson-card`) then a quiz asking each word once, run on the same review engine as SRS with `reviewSource = 'lesson'` (`lessonEntryFor()` builds entries with the same id/shape as `quizEntryFor()`: JP kanji words → reading, Chinese → English meaning with pinyin hint, else meaning in English when the UI isn't Chinese), so lesson answers also grade SRS cards and wrong ones go to the mistake book. `LESSON_PASS_PCT` (80) unlocks the next level; progress is `localStorage.path_progress = {setId: {done, best}}` (`done` = levels passed in a row). quiz.js hands lesson results/again/back to `lessonResultHtml()` / `lessonAgain()` / `lessonBack()`; Starting a lesson sets `last_quiz_set` (home's 繼續上次的 leads with it). `pathAutoOpen` (a level) makes `showSetStart()` open that level's preview as soon as the set loads: home's 練習 N 個單字 with nothing practised yet goes straight to lesson 1 of the learning language's 打招呼 basics (`homeContinue`), and before any topic is started the 主題 tab marks 打招呼 with 下一步 (`.topic-tile.start`). **Romanization (2026-10-25):** Japanese and Korean lesson previews have a 羅馬拼音 switch (`toggleRomaji`, `show_romaji`, per device; `#lesson-tools`) that adds `romanOf()` — Hepburn from the kana (`romajiOf`, こんにちは / こんばんは end in wa) or the Revised Romanization (`hangulRoman`, with liaison, ㄱㄷㅂ + ㄴㅁ nasalised and ㄹㄹ / ㄹㄴ as ll) — under each word, and `quizHintFor()` shows it as the hint of a meaning question (never a reading question); a kana-only word no longer shows itself again as its reading; a set's first level adds a line pointing to the alphabet (`lesson_alpha_tip_ja` / `_ko`). "自由練習" (`showFreePractice`) is the old mode picker, whose "返回" (`modeCardBack`) returns to the path. **On computers (≥1024px, big check 2026-10-24)** the path and the word preview fit one 1080p screen: a unit's levels are a 5-column grid (no zigzag), the unit list scrolls in its own box (`max-height: calc(100vh - 470px)`), and `#lesson-card` is 1180px wide (excluded from the 680px quiz-card rule in css/base.css, listed in rails.js's `wide` so the rails move aside) with the words in two columns (`.lesson-card .lesson-list`).

**Interface icons (`uiIcon(name)` / `UI_ICONS` in js/quiz.js, proposal D 2026-10-17)**: controls use these line icons (speaker, warning, document, book, globe) rather than emoji; emoji stay in content. Also from D: text is at least 12px (0.75rem) except badges and calendar cells, `--text-muted` meets 4.5:1 in both themes, and on touch screens (`pointer: coarse`, css/responsive.css) the small toggles and chips are 44px high.

**Page intros (`introHtml` / `introInfoBtn` in js/quiz.js, 2026-10-17, proposal E)**: a page's one-line intro shows as a tinted box with 知道了 until dismissed, then as an ⓘ beside the title that brings it back (`intro_seen`, per device). Used by 單字 (`words_intro`), 文法 / 閱讀 / 聽解 (`<page>_intro`) and the alphabet (`alpha_hint`); new pages should use it rather than a plain paragraph.
