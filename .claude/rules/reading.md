# Reading pages and exam rounds (TOPIK, JLPT)

(Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-24; keep it up to date when changing these files.)

**Reading (`js/reading.js` + `css/reading.css`, `#page-reading`, added 2026-10-09 — 8 passages each: Japanese N5, N4 (2026-10-16: about twice as long, mixed JLPT formats — stories, an email, notices / flyers to search for information in) and N3 (2026-10-16: ~500 characters, essays and opinion texts with idioms, a letter to a newspaper, two notices; questions on idioms and the writer's point) and N2 (2026-10-16: ~800 characters in JLPT N2 style — two A/B pairs of opposing texts, marked Ａ / Ｂ at the start of their first sentence, proverb-based argument and essays, an information search; questions on what two texts share, what それ refers to, the writer's real view) and N1 (2026-10-16: ~1,000-character argument essays, an A/B pair, an information search; questions on the writer's argument, the reason behind a line, four-character idioms) — owner's plan: each higher level longer and deeper, with harder words and idioms; drafted with the git-ignored tools/.mockups/r_n4.py … r_n1.py, but tools/reading/ja_*.json is the source now, Korean TOPIK I, Chinese HSK 1–2, English / French / Russian / Spanish / German A1, all on the same 8 topics)**: graded passages hand-written in `tools/reading/<id>.json` (original texts only; furigana as `漢字{かな}` = the reading of the kanji run right before it; per paragraph `s` = sentences + `zh` / `en` translation; a glossary `{w, r, zh, en, m}` whose forms `m` are underlined where they appear; 5 `mcq` (4 options, `answer` index, `zh` / `en` explanation quoting the line) + 3 `tf` (`answer` true/false)) and built by `python tools/build_reading.py` into `data/reading/<id>.json` + `index.json`: each sentence becomes segments `[text, furigana, glossary index]` plus its `plain` text and `kana` (recordings are keyed `plain|kana` and spoken from the kana, `build_audio.py`), zh-Hans is added, and the build refuses bare kanji, a glossary word that never appears, a glossary form that cuts a furigana group, wrong question counts, all-same true/false answers; it also prints glossary words outside the level's word lists (`LEVEL_SETS`). Don't edit `data/reading/` by hand. The page: level chips → list → reader (furigana / translation toggles `reading_furi` / `reading_tr`, A− / A+ `reading_font`, a timer, tap a sentence = its recording, 🔊 read-all chains sentences with `speakText`'s `onend`, tap an underlined word = a card with 🔊 and 「加入複習」 (a fresh SRS card)) → one-page quiz (MCQ then ○ / ×, explanation under each; `READING_PASS` 2/3 marks it read in `reading_progress`, synced, best score kept, with the reading time) → 生詞小複習 (each glossary word once, 4 options from the level's other glossary answers; entries from `lessonEntryFor()` filed under `READING_WORD_SET` (ja_n5 → jlpt_n5), graded into `srs_quiz` and the mistake book). Per language: **Chinese** sentences are plain Traditional text in Taiwan wording (or `{t, p}` with hand-written pinyin); the build makes one segment per character `[char, pinyin, glossary, zhuyin, simplified]` with `tools/zh_reading.py` (words it misreads go in `READING_ZH_WORDS`, reading-only so the grammar's readings don't move; review the printed pinyin), glossary `r` is pinyin and gets `simp` / `zy`; passages are translated into English only. The reader draws each character as a column (`.zc`: pinyin / character / zhuyin, punctuation riding in its neighbour's column) and offers 繁 / 简 (`reading_zh_script`, default by UI language) and 拼音 / 注音 / 兩者 / 不顯示 (`reading_zh_rd`, a `data-zhrd` attribute on `#reading-card`). **Russian** is written with a stress mark (U+0301) on every word of 2+ syllables — the build refuses a missing one; glossary `w` without marks, `r` with — and `reading_stress` hides them; recordings and glossary matching use the text without marks. Russian text uses a system font (`[lang="ru"]` rules in css/reading.css and css/grammar.css) because the site's CJK fonts put a combining mark on the next letter. **English** passages are translated into Chinese only and use the browser voice; their review entries go under `en_jh` with the Chinese meaning. Lengths count characters for ja / ko / zh and words elsewhere (`CHAR_LANGS` / `READING_CHAR_LANGS`, `reading_meta_words`). Answers count toward `daily_activity`. Desktop: two columns on a 1180px page (`body.reading-wide`, css/base.css, css/rails.css). **Since 2026-10-24 (big check) the reader fits one 1080p screen on computers:** the tools row sits at the top of the text column, the text scrolls in its own box (`max-height: calc(100vh - 505px)`), the panel is 520px wide and shows one question at a time with number tabs (`readingQPer()` = 1 at ≥1024px, `readingQGo(k)`, `.rd-qtabs` marked ok / no once answered; phones keep `READING_Q_PAGE` (3) a page), panel options use 1rem / 1.85 line height, the 「讀完了嗎？」 hint is hidden there (`.rd-hint`) and 上一篇 / 下一篇 move into the top row (`.rd-top-nav`; the bottom `.rd-bottom-nav` is hidden) — every passage of ja N5–N1 and ko 1급 measured ≤ 936px. **Since 2026-10-22 the questions sit beside the text** (owner's request): the reader's right column (`#rd-panel`, `readingPanelHtml`) has 題目 / 生詞 tabs and shows `READING_Q_PAGE` (3) questions a page with 上一頁 / 下一頁, redrawn alone (`readingRenderPanel`) so the text keeps its scroll; there is no separate quiz view any more (`readingGo('quiz')` means the reader), the timer runs until the last answer, the result sits on top of the panel, 再做一次 / 重新作答 = `readingRetry()`; phones get the panel under the text. Exam rounds show the text left (the wider column, its own scroll box capped by the screen height) and its questions right (`.rdx-cols`); since 2026-10-24 an item's questions are shown one at a time on computers, with number tabs and 下一小題 (`readingExamSub`, `.rdx-q-off` hidden only ≥1024px; phones list them all), short options sit 2 in a row (`.rdx-opts-2`), and the furigana switch heads the questions' column — so every N5 / N4 item fits a 1080p screen without scrolling. Also on computers (big check 2026-10-24) 上一題 / 下一題, a mock's name and 交卷 with the answered count sit in the top row (`.rdx-top-nav` inside `.rd-top-nav`, `.rdx-mock-top`, `.rdx-submit-top`; the bottom nav `.rd-bottom-nav`, `.rdx-mock-row` and `.rdx-submit-bottom` stay for phones), the instruction box and the question column's options are tighter — every question of ja N5–N1 and ko 1–6급 rounds measured ≤ 930px (`__rxMeasure` in tools/.mockups/measure.js). Since 2026-10-25 a level's practice rounds come first and the mock exam is the last card of the same grid (`.ls-mock-item`, its rules in a line under it, `.ls-mock-note`), the 閱讀 and 聽解 lists are four a row on computers with a passage's second line cut to one line and its title to two, so every level's list fits a 1080p screen (≤ 928px). In the 學習 ▾ dropdown and the phone 學習 sheet, tour step `tour_reading_*`, 小花火 line `ctx_reading`.

**Reading exam rounds (`js/reading-exam.js`, 2026-10-18, Korean 1급–6급 in the TOPIK format, owner's plan):** a level file may hold `sets` beside (or instead of) its passages — practice rounds and timed mock exams of short exam-style texts, each with 1–3 four-option questions. Korean: `ko_topik1` (1급, TOPIK I 31–48, 3 rounds × 18 + the 8 old passages as 短文閱讀; the id stayed, the label became 1급), `ko_2` (TOPIK I 49–70, 3 × 22 + the TOPIK I reading mock, 40 questions / 60 min, numbered from 31), `ko_3` (TOPIK II 1–18), `ko_4` (19–31), `ko_5` (32–41), `ko_6` (42–50, 3 × 9 + the TOPIK II reading mock, 50 / 70 min). A set is `{id, mock?, minutes (mock), start (first question number; practice rounds count from 1), items}`; an item is `{type: text|notice|chart|order|insert|headline, ask, text?, notice? {title, rows, lines, foot}, chart? {title, unit, bars}, parts? (order: (가)–(라)), box? (insert: the 〈보기〉 sentence), tr {zh, en}, questions [{ask?, options[4], answer, zh, en}]}`; in `text`, `__x__` underlines, `（　　）` is a blank and ㉠–㉣ are insertion points. `ask` keys map to the exam's Korean instruction (`READING_ASK_KO`) with `reading_ask_<key>` as its translation. `build_reading.py`'s `build_sets()` checks them (4 distinct options, answer, explanations, translation, insertion marks, 4 order parts, minutes on a mock). The content is drafted in the git-ignored `tools/.mockups/ko<N>_*.py` (`ko_rd.py`'s `one()` / `multi()`) and written into `tools/reading/<id>.json` with `python ko_sets.py <id> ko<N>_sets` from that folder (it keeps the file's layout); the JSON is the source. All texts original; keep the answers spread over ①–④ and check that an insertion sentence fits only one place. Practice shows the answer, explanation and translation once picked; a mock runs a clock (submitted at 0), lets picks change until handed in, then an answer sheet opens each question in review. `READING_PASS` of a set right marks it in `reading_progress` as `"<level>:set:<set>"`. **Japanese JLPT-format rounds (2026-10-24, owner's plan A1 B1 C1 D):** each level gets 3 practice rounds + 1 mock, each round the level's real reading section (N5 after the 2020 revision, from jlpt.jp's item table: もんだい４ 2 short texts × 1 question, もんだい５ 1 mid-size text × 2, もんだい６ 1 notice × 1 = 5 questions; the mock 20 minutes — owner's pick B1: N5 20 / N4 30 / N3 40 / N2 50 / N1 60), no recordings (C1), N5 first and the owner reviews each level before the next (D). Drafted in the git-ignored tools/.mockups/ja<N>_sets.py (`short()` / `mid()` / `info()`; N5 written as the papers are, mostly hiragana with full-width spaces between words, only N5 grammar) and written with `python ko_sets.py ja_n<N> ja<N>_sets` (its replacement is a function now, so `
` in texts survives). Every kanji carries its reading `漢字{かな}` (clock times as `9時{じ}`); `build_sets()` turns a Japanese item's `text` (one line per `
`), `notice` fields, `q` and `options` into segments (`text_t`, `notice_t`, `q_t`, `options_t`: `[text, furigana, None, known]`, `ja_segs()`) and keeps the plain text; `known` = every kanji of the run is in `KANJI_LEVEL` up to the level (`known_kanji()`), and such furigana stay hidden until 全部漢字標假名 (`reading_exam_furi` = all; owner's pick A1). Items carry `mondai` (shown as もんだい４), asks `ja_short` / `ja_mid` / `ja_info` (`READING_ASK_JA`, the paper's wording; `reading_ask_ja_*` translate them), options are numbered 1–4 (`READING_OPT_NUMS`). Done: N5, N4 (the 2020 N4 mix: もんだい４ 3 short × 1, ５ 1 mid-size × 3, ６ 1 notice × 2 = 8; mock 30 minutes; ja4_sets.py `place()` moves the right option to `at` so answers spread evenly — explanations never cite option numbers), N3 (unchanged by the 2020 revision: もんだい４ 4 short × 1, ５ 2 mid-size × 3, ６ 1 long × 4 (ask `ja_long`), ７ 1 information page × 2 = 16; mock 40 minutes; drafted in tools/.mockups/ja3_common.py + one file a round, ja3_r1.py … ja3_m1.py, gathered by ja3_sets.py; short texts and information pages lengthened to the official sizes at the owner's request), N2 (unchanged by 2020: もんだい10 5 short × 1, 11 3 mid-size × 3, 12 two texts Ａ / Ｂ × 2 (ask `ja_integrated`; `integ()` writes them under lines that are only Ａ / Ｂ, drawn as `.rdx-ab` boxes), 13 1 long argument × 3, 14 1 information page × 2 = 21; mock 50 minutes; ja2_common.py + ja2_r1.py … ja2_m1.py; written to about the official lengths: short ~200, mid ~450, Ａ+Ｂ ~500, long ~870, information ~650 characters), N1 (もんだい８ 4 short × 1, ９ 3 mid-size × 3, 10 1 long text — essay or story — × 4, 11 Ａ / Ｂ × 2, 12 1 argument × 4 (both long ones ask `ja_long`), 13 1 information page × 2 = 25; mock 60 minutes; ja1_common.py (`argue()` for もんだい12) + ja1_r1.py … ja1_m1.py; about short ~200, mid ~510, long ~950, Ａ+Ｂ ~570, argument ~930, information ~680 characters). At N1 `KANJI_LEVEL` also counts the jouyou kanji the JLPT lists leave out (誰, 鍋, 嘲 …), so only kanji outside the jouyou list get furigana, as on the paper; `ja_segs()` ignores 々 when deciding whether a run is known. Next: the owner reviews N1; then Korean listening. **HSK rounds, Mainland edition (2026-10-30, owner's plan — see memory "HSK 計畫"):** `tools/reading/zh_hsk<N>.json` (level 'HSK 1' …, sets only), written in **Simplified with Mainland wording**, shown in Simplified whatever the site's 繁 / 简 (a Taiwan edition — Traditional, Taiwan wording — comes after the whole Mainland edition; the 8 Taiwan passages of zh_hsk12 stay for it; their level is "TW HSK 1–2", shown as 台灣短文 by learnLevelName() / learn_level_tw_reading so it isn't mistaken for HSK 1). Pinyin is Mainland, from `tools/zh_mainland.py` (words cut longest-first from the HSK lists' Simplified column, whose pinyin has the neutral tones and the r of 儿化; pypinyin for the rest; NEUTRAL particles; 一 / 不 sandhi within a run, 一 kept in numbers and ordinals; `WORDS` for names, places and misreadings — capitals as the papers print them; `NOT_WORDS` for list words that split simple sentences wrongly, 个人; `SINGLE` for a lone character pypinyin reads inconsistently, 谁 shéi) — printed for review by the build like the Taiwan passages'. `build_sets()` makes `text_t` / `q_t` / `options_t` / `bank_t` segments `[char, pinyin, None, hidden]`: HSK 1–2 show pinyin everywhere and a space between words (as the papers), HSK 3+ hide it until 全部顯示拼音 (`reading_exam_furi`, `rdx_py_*`). Explanations quote the line; texts get an English `tr` (bank types none).
