# Speech, recorded audio, recording check, GitHub Actions

(Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-24; keep it up to date when changing these files.)

**English accent (2026-10-16):** `localStorage['en_accent']` (`en-US` default / `en-GB`), set in the settings panel (英文口音) and on the 閱讀 reader (🔊 美式 / 英式, `readingEnTools`); every `speakText(…, 'en…')` is redirected to `enAccent()` unless `opts.exact` (the settings rows), and a text missing in one accent's recordings plays the other's. Recordings: `data/audio/en-US` (Chirp3-HD-Leda) and `en-GB` (Chirp3-HD-Erinome), owner's picks — the full run waits for November's free Chirp3 quota (only test files so far).

**Speech (`js/speech.js`)**: every 🔊 on the site — quiz `speakWord()`, the lesson preview, `dictSpeak()`, `alphaSay()` — calls `speakText(text, lang)`; don't create `SpeechSynthesisUtterance`s elsewhere. Browsers list their old built-in voices first (Windows: Microsoft Ayumi, Heami, Hanhan…), which sound robotic, so `ttsCandidates(lang)` ranks the device's voices: exact region first, then Edge's neural "Natural" voices, Chrome's online "Google …" voices, other online voices, built-in last (Chinese only matches its own region — zh-HK is Cantonese). Visitors can override the pick per language (`TTS_LANGS`, zh-TW and zh-CN separately) in the 設定 panel's voice section (`renderTtsSettings`; the panel was 顯示設定 until 2026-10-22 and is two columns on computers — pages left, voices right; the voices show the two accents and one line naming the languages whose recordings load (`ttsFillRecLine`), with the per-language pickers folded under 進階, re-rendered on `voiceschanged` because Chrome loads voices asynchronously); choices are stored by voice name in `localStorage['tts_voices']` and silently fall back to the automatic pick on a device without that voice. Rate is `TTS_RATE` (0.9). **Chinese accent (2026-10-15):** visitors pick Taiwan or Mainland separately from 繁 / 简 — `localStorage['zh_accent']` (`zh-TW` / `zh-CN`, unset = follow the script on screen: 繁 → Taiwan, 简 → Mainland), set in the settings panel's voice section (`renderTtsSettings`) and by the 🔊 台灣 / 中國大陸 switches on the 閱讀 reader (`readingSetAccent`) and the 文法 examples (`grammarAccentSwitch`). `zhAccent(script)` gives the language code to speak (quiz `speakWord`, the rail's word of the day, the dictionary use it with `speakText`); sentences go through `speakChinese(trad, simp, script, opts)`, which tries both spellings in that accent's index and plays the other accent's recording rather than the browser voice when one is missing. The 注音 alphabet tab stays zh-TW. It's a device setting, not synced. `js/speech.js` loads right after the i18n files.

**Recorded audio (`data/audio/`, built by `tools/build_audio.py`)**: Japanese, Korean and Chinese sound robotic in every browser voice, so the JLPT / TOPIK / HSK words, the topic words (`tp_ja_*`, `tp_ko_*`, `tp_zh_*`), the alphabet chart's ja/ko/zh entries and the voice-picker samples are pre-rendered with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (ja Chirp3-HD-Erinome, ko Chirp3-HD-Kore, zh-CN Chirp3-HD-Leda, zh-TW Wavenet-A — Taiwan Mandarin has no Chirp3 voice; Chirp3 is unreliable on a lone kana / hangul syllable, so those use a Neural2 backup voice). Since 2026-10-08 also French and Russian, since 2026-10-09 Spanish and German (all CEFR and topic words, spoken with their article) and the fr / ru / de / es alphabet tabs, all with Chirp3-HD-Kore (it rolls the Spanish / Russian r properly). German moves to Chirp3-HD-Zephyr (owner's pick 2026-10-11) with `--only de --redo`, which renders into the git-ignored `tools/.audio-redo/de/` and copies the files over the old ones when the run ends (with `--limit` it only makes a test batch) — waiting, like English, for November's free quota. One MP3 per text in `data/audio/<ja|ko|zh-CN|zh-TW|fr|ru|de|es>/<id>.mp3`, where id = cyrb53 of the NFC-normalised text in base 36 — `audioId()` in `js/speech.js` and `audio_id()` in the script must stay identical. `index.json` per language lists the files plus aliases (simplified → traditional Chinese; a Japanese kanji word → its most basic reading). Japanese kanji words are keyed `word|kana` and spoken from the kana, because one word can have several readings (一日); `speakWord()` passes the kana as `opts.reading`. `speakText()` plays a recording when the index has one and the visitor hasn't chosen a browser voice for that language, else falls back to `speechSynthesis` (also when `play()` is blocked or the file is missing offline). **Don't edit `data/audio/` by hand**: after changing a word list or the alphabet data, re-run `python tools/build_audio.py` (needs `pip install lameenc` and `GOOGLE_TTS_KEY` in the git-ignored `tools/.google-tts.env`; existing files are kept, so only new texts are rendered; `--audition` builds a page to compare voices). Words removed from a list leave their old MP3 behind until deleted. **Japanese with Gemini (2026-10-16):** the 閱讀 levels listed in `GEMINI_JA_READING` (ja_n5 … ja_n1 — recorded in that order, `GEMINI_RANK`; the Google pass skips their sentences, so a level waits for Gemini) are recorded by `--gemini --only ja` with Aoede (owner's pick by ear over the Google voice), Gemini reading each sentence as written (`GEMINI_SAY`, kanji and all) rather than the kana; all other Japanese (words, grammar) stays with Google, so run the Google pass first for new glossary words. Note `--gemini` ignores `--dry-run`. **Chinese with Gemini (`--gemini`, 2026-10-10):** Google Cloud has no natural Taiwan Mandarin voice, so the 閱讀 passages and 文法 examples in zh-TW (and in Mainland Mandarin, zh-CN: the 閱讀 passages in Simplified, keyed by the Simplified text, and since 2026-10-15 the 文法 examples, keyed by the Traditional sentence and spoken from its character-by-character `tw2s` conversion; Mainland runs try `GEMINI_LANG_ORDER` — 3.1 first) are re-rendered with Gemini TTS — `GEMINI_TTS_MODELS`: `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` (the owner's pick) first, then `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` and `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` (zh-TW only; it fails in Mainland Mandarin) as each model's 100 requests a day run out; index.json `"gm"` records which model made each file (voice Aoede + an English instruction asking for a Taiwan accent; Kore for Mainland Mandarin — picked by the owner). Key: `GEMINI_TTS_KEY` in the git-ignored `tools/.gemini-tts.env`, from the `hanabi-tts` project (number 257471360524, prepaid Gemini credit — the free tier is only 10 TTS requests a day and paid Tier 1 100 per model; the Cloud trial credit can't pay for Gemini). **The daily run moved to GitHub Actions (2026-10-22, owner's pick, so the PC can stay off):** `.github/workflows/gemini-audio.yml` runs at 08:20 UTC (after the quota resets at midnight US Pacific; 16:20 in Taiwan) or by hand (Actions → Gemini 錄音 → Run workflow), checks out the **`audio-queue` branch**, runs `build_audio.py --gemini --only ja --regemini 3.1-flash-tts-preview`, commits `data/audio` there — never to `main`, so nothing goes live until the owner says Push and `audio-queue` is merged — and posts a report to Discord with `tools/audio_cloud_report.py` (recorded today, lines left, days to go; warns on used-up credit, a failed run, and when everything is done). Repo secrets: `GEMINI_TTS_KEY`, `DISCORD_WEBHOOK`. When the queue is empty, merge `audio-queue` into `main` on the owner's word and disable the workflow. Before that a Windows scheduled task, "Hanabi Gemini 錄音", ran the git-ignored `tools/run_gemini_daily.ps1` (log `tools/.gemini-daily.log`); it needed the PC on and the owner logged in. Gemini appends C2PA content credentials to its PCM, which play as a loud pop — `strip_c2pa()` removes them. About 1 sentence in 5 also ends with a short mouth click (5–15 ms, 10–25 ms after the voice), heard as a small pop after the 。: `drop_end_click()` cuts it from every new clip (picked by the owner by ear over re-rendering, 2026-10-15). The pop the owner heard the moment 🔊 is pressed was something else: the **gemini-3.8 models (flash and flash-lite, not 3.1) start every clip with ~80 ms of broadband noise** (up to about −12 dB, then a gap, then the voice); `drop_lead_burst()` cuts it (it matched all 213 3.8 files, no 3.1 file and 2 of 10,500 Google files, so it only runs on Gemini clips). `--fix-clicks` ran both once over the files made before (from the Gemini originals, at the files' own 22.05 kHz and 48 kbps, since a second 32 kbps encoding audibly dulls them); index.json `"c"` / `"l"` list the files each has been through. Run them **once** per file — some endings are creaky voice (pulses 20 ms apart that each look like a click), and repeated runs would eat into the last syllable. The model sometimes reads the instruction aloud, mostly on lone words, so each clip is checked against its text's length and retried (`gemini_length_ok`); files Gemini made are listed under `"g"` in index.json and kept by later Google runs. `--gemini-all` would also replace the words (≈10k per accent; about $0.0002 a second of audio in 2026, double from 2027).

**Recording check (`tools/audio_check.py` + `tools/audio_check_report.py`, workflow `.github/workflows/audio-check.yml` "錄音檢查", 2026-10-23, owner's picks)**: so nobody has to listen to 100 000 clips. Three layers: (1) coverage — every text the site speaks (`build_audio.jobs()`, plus every object with an audio id `a` in data/listening/) has a file: ok / waiting on `audio-queue` / waiting for its Gemini turn (日文閱讀, tier 0) / missing; (2) health — each MP3 decoded from bytes with miniaudio (`decode_file` can't open a path with 我的網站 in it): silent (peak under `SILENT_DB`), too short / too long for its letters (`letters()` counts every script — build_audio's `spoken_chars()` leaves out hangul and Cyrillic), a quiet gap inside, clipping; (3) content — Gemini (`CHECK_MODEL`, the paid key, `BATCH` clips a request) listens to every sentence and 聽解 line and a fixed 5% of words (`WORD_SAMPLE`) and says whether the clip says its text. Gemini tends to agree when told what to expect, so silence is left to layer 2. Results are kept per file, keyed by its size (a re-recorded file is checked again), in `state.json` on the **`audio-check` branch** (locally in the git-ignored tools/.audio-check/), so each clip is paid for once (about US$1 for everything, 2026-10-23). The workflow runs after 「Gemini 錄音」 / 「Google 錄音」 (workflow_run), weekly on Sundays with every file's health again, or by hand (inputs `content`, `health`); it reads main and audio-queue and never writes them. The report goes to the owner's #錄音檢查 Discord channel (`DISCORD_AUDIO_QA_WEBHOOK`): progress per language (單字 / 句子 / 台詞, waiting, missing), what Gemini checked and what it cost, and each new problem with its MP3 attached (`state.reported`, so a problem is attached once).
